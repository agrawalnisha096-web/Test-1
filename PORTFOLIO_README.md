# Aakash Pareek — Digital Marketing Portfolio (Editable)

**`Aakash_Pareek_Portfolio.pptx`** is a fully editable, professionally redesigned
version of the original `Aakash_Pareek_Portfolio_updated.pdf`.

## What changed

The content and all of your real work (analytics, ad previews, campaign
screenshots, creative samples, certifications, and your avatar) were preserved.
Everything else was rebuilt for a clean, consistent, professional look:

- **One design system** — a single navy / blue / amber palette, consistent
  Calibri typography, and uniform spacing across all 37 slides.
- **Fixed the broken text** — the odd letter-spacing (e.g. "w o r k in g",
  "Ma rket ing") and the mix of serif/sans fonts are gone.
- **Readable layouts** — left-aligned body copy, framed screenshot cards,
  numbered feature grids, and big stat call-outs instead of centered walls of text.
- **Section dividers** — clean numbered section breaks (Social Media Marketing,
  Paid Ads, SEM, More Channels, Creative) replace the casual illustration slides.
- **Consolidated** — 42 pages tightened to 37 focused slides (e.g. the Facebook
  "before/after" is now a single before → after case-study slide).

## How to edit it

Open the `.pptx` in **PowerPoint**, **Google Slides**, or **Canva** and edit any
text, image, or color directly. It is a standard 16:9 PowerPoint file.

Widescreen 16:9 · 37 slides · fonts: Calibri (ships with Office / Google Slides).
