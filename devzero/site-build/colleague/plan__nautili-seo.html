What changed and what's open

  • Done: homepage speed (TTFB 1.68 s → ~0.15 s), large social card, llms.txt and AI-crawler access
  • Biggest open item: the copied DevZero Kubernetes posts are still live, so Google reads Nautili as a Kubernetes site
  • GA4 has collected since at least 11 Oct, but no signup, demo or API-key conversions fire yet, and visits also land in DevZero's GA4
  • Quick wins in Week 1: homepage retitle (30 min), mirror-domain redirects (1 hr), links from devzero.io
  • Search Console: 42 clicks in 3 months, no impressions yet for any target keyword, and no sitemap submitted

How to read the task list

  • P0 next 2 weeks · P1 within 30 days · P2 within 90 days
  • Impact is the expected effect on rankings, AI answers or conversions
  • Verified is what we saw on the live site on 20–22 Nov 2026. Changes that are merged but not deployed show as Pending
