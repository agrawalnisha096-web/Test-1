## Q2. Repeatable methodology (3 min)

*"What exact steps and experiments produced the result? Which experiment worked, how did you measure it, and what SOP did you create?"*

**Steps at Algoscale:**
1. **Built the proof base first:** service pages and case studies that give engines something specific to cite.
2. **Earned third-party presence:** PR placements next to the Tier-1 firms buyers already compare against.
3. **Distributed through communities:** Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn and niche data and AI forums, run as a separate channel.
4. **Measured** AI Overview placements, citations, and the share of pipeline from AI referrals.

**What didn't work:** programmatic location pages ("[service] in [location]") and tool pages ("[service] for [tool]"). They didn't prove effective, so I moved the effort to substantive pages, PR and community distribution.

**Proof it repeats:** I ran the same community-distribution programme at Taggbox/Tagshop (Reddit, Quora and forums across four products: 16K → 60K sessions, 50+ enterprise MQLs a month) and then again at Algoscale.

**The SOP format:** Task 4 of my SoluLab assignment, for getting onto the third-party sources AI engines already cite. It sets out steps, inputs, a QC checklist, fortnightly measurement, and rules for repeating, improving or stopping. I tested it on SoluLab: 45 dated runs showed that SoluLab's only AI recommendation came through two third-party lists.
