**Never write "compass"** (it's an AI-application trap in the posting). The application starts with **"Astro Growth."**

- Same-day spring and cable repair, upfront pricing, 90-day warranty, "12 yrs, 1,800+ doors."

## SEO plays
- Reviews: automatic Housecall Pro request after each job, **15–25/mo per location**. Weekly job photos. Add secondary categories.
- Service pages: springs, cables, off-track, openers, new doors. Make city pages real, **starting with West Chester spring repair** (results are junk: PA and NJ pages).
- Troubleshooting blog (the competitors' traffic source): learn button, sensors, battery backup, "won't close."
- Directories rank page 1 (Yelp, MapQuest, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, Facebook). Local FB groups rank too.
- Schema, click-to-call, Housecall Pro booking widget, matching name/address/phone. Weekly geogrids.

## Local Services Ads: "cost per lead doubled, first 3 checks"
1. **Lead quality:** listen to the recordings and **dispute** spam, sales calls, job seekers, out-of-area, services they don't offer, wrong numbers, repeats (**within 30 days**). Did cost per lead or cost per booked job double?
2. **Settings and auction:** change history (bidding mode, budget, new service areas or job types, hours), new competitors, seasonality.
3. **Ranking signals:** reviews, **missed calls** (they hurt ranking and may be charged), hours, verification.
→ *"Disputes recover money. Fixing settings stops the waste. Judge on cost per booked job against a ~$1,100 ticket."*

## Paid ads
- **Search** = tight exact/phrase on spring, cable, emergency and off-track, call-focused, negatives (DIY, parts, Home Depot, jobs), Housecall Pro booked jobs sent back to Google Ads.
- **Meta** = installs and specials, before-and-after videos, retargeting, push during cold snaps. Every ad gets a matching landing page.
- **Automations:** **missed-call text-back**; new lead → instant text → Housecall Pro → alert the booking team.

## 30/60/90
**Weeks 1–2:** access, profile health, matching contact details, baseline geogrids, CallRail per channel, dashboard, export Local Services Ads history.
**Weeks 3–6:** review engine, profile cleanup, weekly disputes, spam reports, installs page and offer.
**Weeks 7–12:** real city pages, citations, Meta installs, tight Search campaign with offline conversions.

## Ask them
1. Are all your profiles verified and showing?
2. What are budgets, cost per lead and cost per booked job by channel today?
3. Do Housecall Pro outcomes feed back into Google Ads?
4. What's the missed-call rate, and who answers?
5. What's the repair vs install revenue mix?
6. How do you get reviews now?
7. Any expansion plans (Columbus, Northern Kentucky)?
8. How exactly is the bonus measured?
