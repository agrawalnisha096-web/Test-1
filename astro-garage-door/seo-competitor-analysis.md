## 2. Organic search results: who wins

**"garage door repair dayton oh"** and **"garage door repair cincinnati"**: the organic results are mostly **directories**: Yelp, MapQuest, HomeAdvisor, Facebook, Modernize. Very few contractor websites rank.

**"garage door spring repair west chester"**: the results are **junk**. Pages for Plainfield, Munster IN, Newark NJ, Chester PA and West Chester **PA** (not Ohio). Google has no good local page to show.

**Takeaways**
- **There's room for a good West Chester spring repair page.** A real "Garage Door Spring Repair in West Chester, OH" page with local photos, prices or a range, reviews, FAQs and schema could rank quickly.
- **Directory listings rank for them.** A complete Yelp, MapQuest, BBB, Angi/HomeAdvisor, Nextdoor and Facebook presence puts Astro on page 1 even before its own site ranks.
- **Local Facebook groups show up in Google.** Posts in "westchesterliberty" ("reliable garage door repair in west chester?", "who can recommend a good spring repair…") rank on page 1. Being active in community groups and having customers recommend you there is real visibility.

3. **The homepage wins the main city search.** Dayton Door Sales ranks **#1 for "dayton garage door repair"** with its homepage. Astro's homepage is Dayton-focused, but its Dayton profile isn't visible, so the two don't support each other.

## 4. SEO talking points for the interview

1. **"Your map pack problem is a profile problem, not a website problem."** AMR outranks you with almost no website. Reviews (4 vs. 145+), photos (9 vs. 100–300), secondary categories and profile health close the gap.
2. **Fix the base first:** confirm the Dayton profile is live and verified, merge the possible duplicate West Chester listing, and make the name, address and phone match everywhere.
3. **Review engine:** an automatic Housecall Pro request after each job, naming the technician, aiming for 15–25 a month per location. It feeds both the map pack and Local Services Ads ranking.
4. **Photo cadence:** technicians upload before-and-after photos to each profile weekly. The same photos go into Google profile posts, Facebook/Instagram and the website.
5. **Service pages:** spring repair, cable repair, off-track, opener repair and installation, plus new garage doors and openers (LiftMaster, if they're a dealer). Today the site has none.
6. **Make the city pages real, starting with West Chester.** Spring repair in West Chester has junk results: low competition, high intent ($110 CPC). Add real job photos, neighborhoods, local reviews, a price range and FAQs. Merge or rewrite the 17 copied city pages.
7. **Troubleshooting blog**, the competitors' proven play: learn button, sensors, battery backup, "garage door won't close", "spring broke, what now", "repair vs replace", "spring repair cost in Dayton/Cincinnati". Each post ends with "Not working? Same-day repair → call."
8. **Directory presence:** Yelp, MapQuest, BBB, Angi/HomeAdvisor, Nextdoor and Facebook rank on page 1 for city searches. Complete them and keep business info consistent.
9. **Community:** local Facebook groups (West Chester/Liberty) rank in Google. Encourage happy customers to recommend Astro there, and answer questions helpfully as the business page.
10. **Schema and the technical basics on Framer:** LocalBusiness/HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema with name, address, phone and service areas, Service and FAQ markup, reviews, click-to-call, fast mobile pages, and an online booking widget (Housecall Pro).
11. **Spam fighting:** report fake or duplicate listings above them (for example "Man VS Door" listed twice in Dayton, and the zero-review "Prime Choice" ranking #1 in Cincinnati) through Google's Business Redressal form.
12. **Measure:** a weekly geogrid per location on 5–6 core searches, plus Google Search Console clicks, calls from the profiles, and booked jobs by source.
