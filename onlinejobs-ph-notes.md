# OnlineJobs.ph — Understanding the Site

## What it is
OnlineJobs.ph is the largest online marketplace for finding and hiring Filipino
virtual assistants (VAs) and remote employees. Founded in 2008 by John Jonas, it
hosts over 2 million worker profiles and functions primarily as a job board +
resume database dedicated to Filipino remote workers.

## Business model
- **Zero-commission**: Employers pay a flat monthly subscription (starting around
  $69/month) to contact workers. They then hire and pay workers directly at a
  negotiated rate — no per-hire percentage fees, no salary markups.
- Searching and filtering profiles is free; **payment is only required to contact**
  workers.
- The platform connects employers and candidates but does **not** screen or vet
  candidates — the employer owns the whole hiring process.

## Two sides of the marketplace
- **Employers**: Post jobs and search the worker/resume database to find candidates.
- **Job seekers (VAs)**: Create profiles and search/apply for jobs.

## How search works
Two main search surfaces:

1. **Job search (for job seekers)** — e.g.
   `https://www.onlinejobs.ph/jobseekers/jobsearch?jobkeyword=Virtual+Assistant`
   - Keyword-based search over job postings.
   - Category browse pages, e.g.
     `https://www.onlinejobs.ph/jobseekers/search/c/virtual-assistant`.

2. **Worker/resume search (for employers)** — a filterable resume database:
   - Filter by **date** the listing was created
   - Filter by **skills** required
   - Filter by **skill level**
   - Filter by **desired salary**

## Common job categories
Virtual assistants, Graphic Design, Social Media Marketing, SEO, Content Writing,
AI, Customer Support, E-Commerce.

## Typical rates
Filipino VAs are commonly hired at roughly **$300–$800/month**, with no ongoing
platform commission on their salary.
