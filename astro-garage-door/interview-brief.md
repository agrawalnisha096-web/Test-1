**Platform changes worth mentioning** (shows you're current)

Before migration, the standalone LSA dashboard history goes away, so **export lead history and dispute records now**.
- Google's new "pre-badge" LSA ads are **not available for garage door** (it's an advanced-verification category), so a clean background check, licensing and insurance on file matter.
- Search results now show **AI Overviews** above the organic results (seen live on the West Chester search). The map pack and LSA sit above everything else, which makes local SEO and LSA more valuable, not less.

## 6. The screening question: "LSA cost per lead doubled last month. First 3 things you'd check?"

A strong 3-part answer:

1. **Lead quality: are we paying for junk?** Open the lead log. Look for spam, wrong services (commercial doors, gates), out-of-area leads, repeat callers, job seekers, and sales calls. **Dispute them** (there's a window, so do it weekly). Also check whether the lead mix shifted (more message leads, fewer calls) and whether "cost per lead" or "cost per *booked job*" is the number that actually doubled.
2. **Settings and competition: what changed in the auction?** Check the change history: bidding mode (manual max-per-lead vs. automated), budget, newly added **service areas** or **job types** (a new city or commercial work can pull expensive leads), business hours. Then look at competitors: a new player, PMax migration or seasonal demand (cold snap → spring peak) raising prices.
3. **Ranking signals and responsiveness on our side.** LSA ranking weighs **review count and rating, responsiveness (answered calls, reply speed), proximity and hours**. If calls went unanswered or reviews stalled, we rank lower and pay more for what we do get. Check CallRail for missed calls and confirm the profile and verification are healthy.

Close with: *"Then I'd decide: dispute, tighten areas or job types, fix responsiveness, or accept a higher CPL if booked-job cost is still profitable against a ~$1,100 average ticket."*

## 7. Smart questions to ask them

1. How many GBP locations do you have, and are they all verified and showing? (Mention the Dayton brand-search gap.)
2. What are current monthly budgets by channel, and what are CPL and cost per booked job today?
3. Is Housecall Pro job status passed back to Google Ads/LSA (offline conversions)? Who updates job outcomes?
4. Who answers the phones, and what's the missed-call rate after hours?
5. What's the revenue split between repair and installs? Which do you want more of?
6. How do you get reviews today? Is the HCP automatic request turned on?
7. Expansion plans: Columbus? Northern Kentucky? (Columbus CPCs are ~$100, so expansion should lead with GBP and LSA, not Search.)
8. How is the bonus measured: which ranking grids and keywords, and how is "booked job" defined?
