Dominic,

Based on our discussions, the details in your job post and a few assumptions of my own, I'd like to propose working with Qubik as its part-time marketing lead for both businesses: Qubik AI & Automation and Qubik Accounting. I'd own lead generation, marketing support and the email, SMS and CRM automation that turns leads into revenue.

The role you advertised covers the AI side. Much of the early work, though, sits with the accounting business: the 3,800 existing leads, the tax-debt campaign and new Meta and Google campaigns. Having one person across both means shared tools, one way of reporting, and no gaps between the two.

I'd rather not price this task by task. That would stop me from picking up what comes up or testing new ideas. Instead, I'm proposing a fixed monthly retainer for a clear set of priorities plus day-to-day support, and commission on revenue I bring in.

## How I'd work

I'd work like a member of your team, not an outside supplier, for about 27–35 hours a week during Brisbane business hours.

- **Priorities:** at the start of each month we agree a short ranked list for each business. That list gets most of my time.
- **Day-to-day support:** I pick up what comes up between priorities. If something urgent arrives, we agree what moves down the list.
- **Outside help:** when a task needs a designer, video editor or other specialist, I find them, brief them and manage the work. You approve the cost first and pay them directly, with no markup from me.
- **Ownership:** every account, ad account, page, list and file stays in Qubik's name. I work inside your systems with partner or user access.
- **Working with your team:** sales handles calls and closing, and your automation specialist handles technical builds. I make sure leads reach them ready to act on.

## Priorities for the first 90 days

These are my suggested starting priorities. We'd confirm and re-rank them at our first monthly call.

### Qubik AI & Automation

1. Meta and Google lead generation: launch, test, and scale what brings qualified meetings.
2. Landing pages, tracking and the hand-off of every lead into the CRM for sales.
3. Lead follow-up by email and SMS, lead scoring, and clear rules for when sales picks a lead up.
4. Sales materials: a sample diagnostic report and a short demo of a management dashboard.
5. Case studies from the first clients, once they agree.
6. A light LinkedIn presence, and support for reaching larger companies.

### Qubik Accounting

1. Reactivating the 3,800 existing leads from November to January, around Black Friday, Christmas and the New Year.
2. Improving the tax-debt campaign: faster first contact, better segments, follow-up, qualifying and booking.
3. New Meta and Google campaigns for core accounting services.
4. A monthly email to engaged contacts, and preparing the April–June end-of-financial-year campaign.
5. Asking happy clients for Google reviews and referrals.

## Working rhythm and reporting

- **Monthly:** a 45-minute call with you to review results, agree the priorities for each business and set the time split.
- **Weekly:** a short report for each business every Monday, taken from the CRM: spend, leads by source, qualified leads, cost per qualified lead, meetings held, proposals, new clients and cash collected, plus what I'm changing next week.
- **Quarterly:** a review of goals, results, the retainer and the time split.
- **Day to day:** available during Brisbane business hours on your usual messaging and email, and on calls with sales or your automation specialist as needed.

### Reactivated leads: choose one option

The ₹1,50,000 accounting part covers the reactivation work. Because the 3,800 leads already belong to Qubik, you can choose how results from them are paid during the initial 3-month term.

|  | Option A: fixed | Option B: performance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly retainer | ₹4,50,000 (₹2,00,000 for Accounting) | ₹4,00,000 (₹1,50,000 for Accounting) |

A **free review** is the free 30-minute online review offered in the campaign: a look at the person's tax position, cash flow and structure for the year ahead. It counts when they attend and are someone Qubik could take on as a client.

As an illustration, if the campaign brings 6–14 new clients at an average first-year fee of A$4,000, Option B would cost about ₹2–4 lakh over the three months, against ₹1.5 lakh extra for Option A. We'd confirm the average fee together.

### How commission works

- It is paid only on cash Qubik has collected, and refunded if Qubik refunds the client.
- The CRM's first-source field decides which leads are mine.
- It applies to deals that close within 6 months of a lead's first contact, including after the contract ends.
- There is no cap, as in your job post. It is calculated monthly and invoiced with the next retainer.

**Outside costs:** ad spend, software and outside contractors are approved and paid by Qubik directly. I add no markup.

**Term:** an initial contract of 3 months, with the retainer guaranteed for that period. At the end we review the results together, then either renew for another 3 months or move to a rolling monthly arrangement with 30 days' notice on either side. I'd work as an independent contractor and invoice in Indian rupees.
