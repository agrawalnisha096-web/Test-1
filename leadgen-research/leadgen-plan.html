Abstrakt has 1,147 industry × city pages, and its homepage earns 75%. The pages that do earn traffic are trade hubs: Scorpion /hvac/, 33 Mile Radius /hvac-leads/, 99 Calls /Plumbing-Leads.htm. I
  4. Google Local Services Ads is the largest single topic: 89,000 searches a month across 37 keywords. Contractors researching LSA are exactly our buyer, and the current top results are small agencies' blog posts. V
  5. Results pages are full of AI, video and Reddit. Of 98 checked keywords, 97 show an AI Overview, 56 have YouTube in the top 10, 26 Facebook, 21 Reddit, and 14 a local pack. Pages need to answer first with concrete numbers, and a YouTube and Reddit presence is needed alongside the site. V
  6. Positioning gap. Only 99 Calls and Service Direct publish per-lead prices. Agencies charge $2K–8K a month on 12-month contracts. Published per-trade pricing, no contract and clear billing rules would stand out in every comparison. That's your call (see §8). V

Rules

  • Each keyword goes to exactly one page, so pages don't compete with each other.
  • A child page only gets built if its cluster is ≥500 searches a month. Otherwise it's a section on the hub.
  • No city/state pages at launch. Test 5 city pages for the 2 best trades after month 3, and expand only if they rank.
  • Internal links: every hub links to its children, /pricing/, the LSA page and its comparison page. Guides link up to their hub. Breadcrumbs everywhere.
  • Schema: Organization + Service on hubs, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList. Pricing in HTML tables so AI Overviews can quote them.

Trade hub template (built ~55 times)

  1. H1 "Exclusive {Trade} Leads & Marketing"
  2. Answer-first block (40–60 words): cost per lead, what's billable, how fast leads start
  3. Per-trade data table: our price, Google Ads CPC (e.g. roofing ≈ $57/click), LSA cost, close rate
  4. Channels for this trade (SEO, LSA, Ads), linked to child pages
  5. Sample lead, anonymised
  6. Trade case study or testimonial
  7. FAQ, 4–6 questions from the People Also Ask boxes (they show on 86 of 98 checked keywords)
  8. Form with trade preselected + click-to-call
  9. Embedded YouTube video, 2–4 minutes

Conversion setup

Step 1: trade, ZIP, phone, email. Step 2: name, company, monthly lead goal, current lead sources. A sticky click-to-call bar on mobile. GA4 key events form_start, form_submit, call_click and pricing_view, plus call tracking. Competitor forms range from 1 field (99 Calls) to 10 (Modernize).

07Risks

  • Fulfilment comes before marketing. Selling exclusive leads needs a way to produce them: your own lead sites, LSA/Ads management, or buying calls. If fulfilment lags, reviews suffer fast.
  • Comparison pages must be factual: use competitors' published terms, date-stamp them, no trademarked logos.
  • LSA cluster is mostly informational, and Google's help pages and big agencies compete. Treat it as a branding and link magnet, not quick revenue.
  • Solar is crowded and high-CPC with heavy TCPA exposure. SMS and call consent must be TCPA-compliant for every trade.
  • AI Overviews on 97% of results pages cut clicks on informational queries, which is why the plan leans toward commercial pages.

08Decisions needed

  1. Brand name and domain.
  2. Offer: exclusive pay-per-lead (like 99 Calls), pay-per-call (like Service Direct), retainer agency (like Hook), or a hybrid. This sets the copy on /pricing/ and every hub.
  3. Fulfilment: how the leads are produced.
  4. Tech stack: I'd recommend Astro or Next.js (static) on Vercel with Markdown content, so the ~55 hubs come from one data file. WordPress is fine if non-developers will edit.
