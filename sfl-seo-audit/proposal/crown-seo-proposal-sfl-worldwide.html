02 · Major issue

The international pages

Critical · biggest revenue gap

SFL's international business is almost invisible in search

International shipping and moving are SFL's core business. SFL has 51 country shipping pages and 6 country moving pages, yet in search results it appears only for India. Its car shipping page covers US domestic transport only.

  • Templated country pages. With the country name swapped out, the New Zealand and Saudi Arabia pages share 68% of their text, Haiti and Jamaica 56%, and Denmark and Finland 54%. The same H2s appear on up to 51 pages.
  • Stale rates. The Pakistan and Nigeria pages show "rates verified and updated in November 2023". The UK, Canada, Pakistan and Nigeria pages don't mention 2026 at all.
  • Missing answers. The UK page lacks price per lb or kg and the UK address format. The Canada page lacks the de minimis threshold and dimensional weight. Competitor pages that rank cover both.
  • No international car shipping page. 686 keywords, including "how much does it cost to ship a car overseas" and "international car shipping companies", have nowhere to land. SFL's post that ranked #5 for these searches now returns 410.
  • Missing moving markets. There are no moving pages for Dubai, the UK, Canada, Nigeria, the Philippines or South Africa; 230 core moving keywords have no page.
  • Missing corridors. There are no pages for Sri Lanka or Bangladesh, both core South Asian routes.
  • Locale copies. 31 of the 72 pages in /en-in/ and /en-ca/ have no hreflang, 12 page pairs across locales are identical, and /en-in/blog is empty.

Who wins these searches instead: Easyship (14 of 40 queries), Parcel Monkey (13), DHL (11), Meest (9) and Shippo (7). They lead with a live rate calculator, a price in the search result and data dated 2026.

03 · Full audit

Every finding, ranked

CriticalService pages canonicalised to the homepage

EvidenceThe FedEx, DHL, UPS, USPS and Domestic Shipping pages (1,255–2,629 words each) declare https://www.sflworldwide.com as their canonical URL, and their hreflang tags point to the homepage. Domestic Shipping is linked from 382 of 383 US pages. ImpactGoogle is told not to index SFL's carrier-discount pages, its core offer. 373 carrier keywords such as "fedex shipping to india from usa cost" have no indexable page. FixSelf-referencing canonicals and correct hreflang on all five pages.

Critical144 retired URLs with no redirect

EvidenceOf the 509 URLs in /llms.txt, 136 return 410 and 8 return 404. /blog/is-it-worth-shipping-tv-from-usa-to-india still ranks #3. Live pages link to 34 dead URLs (58 links), 8 of them on the HTML sitemap page. ImpactEvery retired URL's rankings and backlinks are thrown away, and visitors hit error pages. Fix301-redirect each retired URL to its closest live page; repair the 58 links; rebuild llms.txt.

CriticalBlog posts that links don't reach

Evidence61 sitemap pages can't be reached through links, 52 of them blog posts. Another 146 posts are 4 or more clicks deep; the deepest is 51 clicks from the homepage. /blog lists 9 posts behind "Load More", and /blog?page=2 returns the same 9. robots.txt disallows /blog/page/. Impact246 posts pass and receive almost no internal link value. FixCrawlable pagination, topic hubs and cross-links, so every post sits within 3 clicks of the homepage.

HighContent that doesn't answer the search

EvidenceBlog posts carry 8.1 figures per 1,000 words, against 10.7–55.8 on competitor blogs. "How Long Does It Take to Ship from the USA to India?" (1,657 words) gives no day count. 9 of 41 cost-titled posts contain no price. On all 246 posts, "Last updated" equals the publish date. ImpactSearchers leave for competitors that give the number. FixPut the answer, with a figure, at the top of every page; add dated rate tables; real update dates.

HighNo structured data on the blog; incomplete entity data

Evidence202 of 455 pages carry JSON-LD, and 0 of 246 blog posts do. The 9 Organization blocks have no sameAs. There's no LocalBusiness markup for the 5 offices. One FAQ answer is empty (Australia). The default share image is 211×153 px on 438 pages. ImpactSearch engines and AI assistants can't reliably identify SFL's articles, authors or offices. FixBlogPosting, BreadcrumbList, Person, Organization, WebSite and LocalBusiness markup in every template.

HighHeavy, uncached pages

EvidenceMedian HTML is 784 KB (homepage 852 KB); competitors' median is 156 KB. HTML is sent no-store and never cached. Mobile LCP was 5.2–8.7 s on 5 of 6 pages tested. Google Tag Manager is 522 KB. The 1,200×1,200 author photos (612 KB and 754 KB) load at full size on every post. The homepage logs React hydration error #418. ImpactSlow pages on mobile, where most of SFL's international customers search. FixEdge caching, image optimisation, a leaner tag load, and the hydration fix.

MediumSitemap, robots and templates

Evidence435 of 455 sitemap lastmod values share one date. The sitemap lists a noindex page and the 5 canonicalised pages. The 404 page outputs both noindex and index, follow. /worldwide-moving is titled "worldwide-moving". track. and pay. are open to crawlers with generic titles. FixReal lastmod dates, a clean sitemap, a single-robots 404 page, corrected titles, noindex on app subdomains.

MediumTrust and local signals

EvidenceOffice labels don't match their addresses: "Atlanta" is in McDonough, "Los Angeles" in Santa Ana, "San Francisco" in Fremont. Pages state 15, 18 and 20 years in business. The Trustpilot rating (4.2 from 520 reviews) isn't shown on the site. 2% of posts cite an official source. FixConsistent office names and addresses (NAP) everywhere; one years-in-business figure; a live review widget; citations to customs authorities.

05 · Scope of work

What we will provide

Technical SEO, implemented

Our team writes and tests every code change and submits it to SFL's repository for approval. The work covers:

  • canonical and hreflang fixes;
  • redirects and broken-link repair;
  • the sitemap, robots.txt and 404 page;
  • crawlable blog pagination and topic hubs;
  • caching headers, image optimisation and the hydration fix;
  • a re-crawl after every release to confirm it worked.

Content: 12–15 pieces a month

A monthly mix of new pages, rewrites and blog posts, each built around a mapped keyword and SFL's real rates:

  • New pages: the international car shipping hub and routes, moving pages, and Sri Lanka and Bangladesh.
  • Rewrites: country pages, starting with Nigeria, the Philippines, Pakistan, the UK, Canada, Ghana and the UAE.
  • Blog posts: the question bank (cost, transit time, prohibited items, moving guides) and the 10 posts that currently don't answer their own title.

Foundational backlinks

  • Link reclamation: backlinks pointing to the 144 retired URLs recovered through redirects, plus unlinked brand mentions in press and award listings converted to links.
  • Citations: consistent business profiles for the 5 offices across Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, BBB, Yelp and logistics directories.
  • Associations: member-profile links from the bodies whose badges SFL shows (IAM, FIDI, ARA, IMA).
  • Community outreach: Indian, Nigerian, Filipino and Pakistani diaspora organisations, and expat and relocation publications.
  • Data asset: a quarterly "shipping cost index" built from SFL's own rates, pitched to journalists and bloggers.

AEO: answer engine optimisation

Getting SFL cited in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini:

  • answer-first page structure: a direct answer with a number in the first 60 words;
  • FAQ and comparison tables built from the question bank;
  • consistent entity data: Organization sameAs, office addresses, authors with credentials;
  • a rebuilt llms.txt and a review of AI crawler access in robots.txt;
  • citations to customs authorities on rule-based content;
  • monthly tracking of SFL's presence in AI answers for 30 target prompts.

Structured data

Article, author, breadcrumb, organization, website and office-location markup, built into the templates so every new page inherits it.

Keyword targeting

A keyword-to-page map covering the 3,355-keyword universe, one main keyword per page, and a 330-question bank for FAQs and blog posts.

Tracking and reporting

Weekly rank tracking on the 23 main keywords. A monthly report on work shipped, rankings, organic traffic, links gained and AI citations, with a review call.

One named strategist

A single senior contact for the whole engagement, reachable by email between monthly calls.
