# AI venture: name and domain shortlist (22 Nov 2026)

**Brief:** a global AI consulting and implementation brand from Mosta International, founded by the team behind Qubik. It needs to be credible with owners, CEOs and CFOs, easy to say in any language, and ownable as a .com (main site) plus .ai.

## How the search was done
- About 380 candidate names, built in several ways:
  - Mosta-based names;
  - Maltese words and places (the Mosta link);
  - building and craft metaphors;
  - invented brand names;
  - two-word benefit names.
- **.com:** checked against the Verisign registry (exact answer: registered or not).
- **.ai:** the registry rate-limited bulk lookups, so .ai was checked by DNS. A name with no DNS record is *probably* unregistered, but confirm at the registrar.
- Leading names were web-searched for existing companies with the same name.

## What we learned
- **Every one-word English or invented name tested is taken as a .com** (200+ tried, for example metis, praxio, cupola, lodestar, steward, opslane, vantara, solvara). Almost all of them are also taken as .ai.
- **A distinctive name for the business, or a name built on "Mosta", is the realistic route** to owning both .com and .ai.
- **Mosta, Malta, has about 128 registered organisations** (Crunchbase), so a trademark search on any "Mosta" name is essential.

## Shortlist (.com free on the registry; .ai probably free)

| # | Name | Domains | Why | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mosta Works** | mostaworks.com, mostaworks.ai | Uses the registered company name. "Works" means delivery and results, which fits consulting and implementation. Easy anywhere. | Trademark check, because of other Mosta businesses |
| 2 | **Melitai** | melitai.com, melitai.ai | One distinctive word: *Melita*, the ancient name of Malta, plus *AI*. It carries the Mosta and Malta story. No company of this name found. | Pronunciation ("MEL-ih-tie") needs showing once. Close to Meliva AI and Melior AI. |
| 3 | **Rotunda Works** | rotundaworks.com, rotundaworks.ai | Mosta's landmark Rotunda (the dome): solid, well built, memorable. No company of this name found. | Longer. The story needs telling. |
| 4 | **Mosta Labs** | mostalabs.com, mostalabs.ai | Good if AI products are part of the plan | Many similar "M… Labs" AI firms |
| 5 | **Mosta Engine** | mostaengine.com, mostaengine.ai | Suggests automation doing the work | More product-like than consulting |
| 6 | **Hidma** | hidma.ai, hidmaworks.com | Maltese for "work". Short. | hidma.com is taken. Similar to Hikma (Arabic for "wisdom"). |

**Benefit names, better for campaigns or offers than the company:** Work Unloaded (workunloaded.com and .ai), Lifted Load (liftedload.com and .ai).

**Rejected:**
- Engine Room AI: Engineroom.io already sells ML and AI tools.
- Single English words: all taken.
- Dawl Works and Cupola Works: hard to say, or weak meaning.

## Recommendation
- **Mosta Works** as the company name: the safest and clearest, and it carries the registered name.
- **Melitai** if Dominic wants a single distinctive word with a story.
- Register **.com, .ai and .com.au together** as soon as one is chosen.
- Run trademark searches with IP Australia, WIPO (UK, US) and EUIPO (Malta and the EU) before printing anything.
