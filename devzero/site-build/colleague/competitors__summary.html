Cast AI wins on volume, not quality

10,315 US keywords, but 85% sit on pages 6–10. Its biggest traffic page is a Google Cloud pricing post, not a buyer query. The real gap is links (3,735 domains) and proof (42 case studies).

ScaleOps is beatable in search

Only 232 ranking keywords and 161 indexed URLs. Its blog has slowed to 1–4 posts a month since July, and it blocks GPTBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended, so AI answers can't read it. It wins on LinkedIn spend.

DevZero ranks, but for the wrong queries

458 top-10 keywords, almost all cloud pricing and instance pages. Not in the top 100 for "kubernetes cost optimization", and no Google ads.

Nautili's rivals own the brand searches

LiteLLM gets 27,100 searches a month, Portkey 5,400, Vercel AI Gateway 2,900. Nautili has compare pages for only Portkey and OpenRouter.

Fastest wins

  • DevZero: own "rightsizing without restarts". No vendor ranks on page 1 for it today, and it's DevZero's headline claim
  • Nautili: own the AI-coding-cost queries: "claude code pricing" (33,100 a month, $3.09 a click) and "cursor pricing" (9,900, $10.68). Weak competition
  • Both: AI answers. DevZero and Nautili allow AI crawlers; ScaleOps blocks them and Cast AI's llms.txt is thin
