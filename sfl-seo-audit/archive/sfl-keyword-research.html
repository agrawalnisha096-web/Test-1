02Method and limits

  1. Seeds. 213 seed phrases built from SFL's services and 32 destination countries: shipping, courier, cargo, moving and car shipping to each country from the USA, plus service terms such as package forwarding, freight forwarder and carrier names.
  2. Expansion. Each seed went to Google Autocomplete (US, English) and Bing Autosuggest. The 25 most important seeds were also run with every letter a–z appended. That made 863 Google requests and returned 6,956 unique suggestions.
  3. Filtering. Suggestions unrelated to shipping or moving were removed: travel, visas, tracking numbers, marketplaces such as Temu or Depop, money transfer. 6,082 relevant keywords remained.
  4. Classification. Each keyword was tagged by country, shipping direction, service cluster and intent, then given an SFL fit tier. Core means outbound from the US, or a generic international service SFL sells.
  5. Demand signal. A relative score built from how many seed queries surfaced the keyword, whether both Google and Bing suggested it, and how high it appeared in the suggestion list. It isn't search volume. It ranks keywords against each other. Strong is a score of 9 or more (top 3%, 103 keywords), Moderate is 4.1–9 (740), Weak is below 4.1.
  6. Competition. Judged from the 52 results pages collected for this project: the 40 from the competitor analysis and 12 new ones for the biggest gaps.
  7. Mapping. Each keyword was matched to the SFL page that should rank for it (by service and country rules), to the closest live post, and to any deleted post with a closer match.

No search volumes. This environment has no access to Google Keyword Planner, Semrush or Ahrefs, and Google Trends refused requests (429). Before committing budget, pull volume and difficulty for the priority keywords in section 7 from one of those tools. The clusters and the page map won't change much; the order within them might.

08Excluded keywords

Keep these out of targeting and paid campaigns. They showed up in the suggestions but don't match what SFL sells.

  • Tracking and carrier accounts: "fedex tracking", "usps store hours", carrier logins.
  • Competitor brand names: Meest, Shipito, MyUS, Planet Express, Allied and others (127 keywords).
  • Marketplace shipping: Temu, Depop, Shein, Etsy, eBay seller rules.
  • Money transfer: "send money to India", remittance tax.
  • Travel: flights, visas, "travelling to Nepal from USA".
  • Shipments that neither start nor end in the US: "shipping from UK to Switzerland", "India to Australia container".
  • Local businesses abroad: "courier jamaica opening hours", named movers in Hong Kong.

2,600 lower-fit keywords stay in the CSV, tagged "Low fit", for reference.
