What we found

Nautili

Fast now, but Google still reads it as a Kubernetes blog

  • 42 clicks from Google in 3 months, and no impressions yet for any keyword it wants
  • 39 of its 46 blog posts are copies of devzero.io posts, so Google treats it as a Kubernetes site. Removing them is task one
  • Biggest opening: what Claude Code costs. "claude code pricing" and "claude code cost" get 38,500 US searches a month, at $3–4 a click
  • Rivals own their brand searches: LiteLLM 27,100 a month, Portkey 5,400. Nautili has comparison pages for 2 of 8 rivals
  • Its 584 model and provider pages carry about 190 words each, and its homepage tells Google the company is "DevZero"
  • Rivals grow through distribution: Requesty has 20 integration pages and ~800 linking sites; Nautili has 1 and 6. Listings in coding tools, marketplaces and directories come next
  • Several "new" pages partly exist: Cursor, Codex and Continue set-up is in the docs, the Gemini CLI guide exists, the OpenRouter fee calculator sits inside /compare/openrouter. They become upgrades, not new builds
DevZero

It ranks, but mostly for cloud pricing, not for buyers

  • 6,010 clicks in 3 months. The AKS, GKE and EKS cost posts sit at positions 5–8 with ~1,000 impressions each and no clicks: new titles are a 4-hour fix
  • Buyers search competitor names: "cast ai" 1,900 a month, "scaleops" 1,300, "kubecost" 880. DevZero has compare pages but no "alternatives" pages yet
  • No vendor ranks on page 1 for "rightsizing without restarts", DevZero's headline claim, and DevZero's own /pod-rightsizing page has no links pointing to it
  • Outside its site, DevZero is still described as a dev-environment company (Gartner files it under code-editor software)
  • Slow on phones: Google's speed test scores the homepage 54/100, with main content taking 4.5 s to appear (good is under 2.5 s)
  • Its own pages compete with each other: three AKS pricing posts rank for the same search, and links point at the weaker one. Merging fixes it in hours
  • llms.txt, which AI engines read, still leads with MicroVMs and links legacy dev-environment pages. The fix is ready and takes an hour
  • 18% of the last 12 months' clicks (3,623) came from 47 old URLs that now redirect to /blog. Restoring the top 9 protects about 300 clicks a month
  • Pricing guides and instance pages are seen millions of times but clicked 0.25% of the time; prices in titles are the fastest traffic win

Before any ad spend

Nautili: $3,000/mo, 4 campaigns

  • GA4 conversions for signup, demo booked and first API key (GA4 itself already collects)
  • Nautili visits kept out of DevZero's GA4
  • Google Ads conversion tag and a remarketing audience
  • Landing pages live: Claude Code cost calculator, /compare/litellm, new homepage title
  • Mirror domains redirected to nautili.ai

DevZero: $5,300/mo US, 5 campaigns

  • Google Ads conversions for demo booked and sign-up (GA4 already records both), and Nautili visits kept out of DevZero's GA4
  • Remarketing audience from the same tag
  • /alternatives pages for Cast AI, ScaleOps and Kubecost
  • The rightsizing pillar page live

EU ($1,900) and India ($850) follow from Week 3, copying the campaigns that convert.
