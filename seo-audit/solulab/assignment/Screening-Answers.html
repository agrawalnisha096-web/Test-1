Talking points for each timed question. Every figure comes from my portfolio or my SoluLab assignment.

"Show us one campaign where you personally improved a company's visibility in AI-generated answers for commercial queries…"

Algoscale Technologies, data and AI consulting (Dec 2024-present).

  • Context: Algoscale competes for the same buyers as Accenture, TCS and Deloitte. There was no paid search, and I built the SEO function from zero.
  • What I did personally: ran the AEO/GEO programme across 200+ target queries for Algoscale's data and AI consulting services. That meant 100+ service pages, 25 case studies, a full site rebuild, PR placements alongside Accenture, TCS and Deloitte, and a community programme on Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn and niche data communities.
  • AI visibility results:
    • 50+ placements in Google AI Overviews.
    • Consistent citations in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, alongside Accenture, TCS and Deloitte.
  • Business results:
    • 0 → 65K monthly sessions in 12 months.
    • $1M+ pipeline in 6 months and $450K revenue realised, organic only.
    • More than 10% of monthly pipeline from AI referrals.
  • What I can show: Search Console screenshots (22.2K clicks and 6.4M impressions; top service page 9.67K clicks at 1.2% CTR; top transactional blog 8.61K clicks at position 5.6).

Q2. Repeatable methodology (3 min)

"What exact steps and experiments produced the result? Which experiment worked, how did you measure it, and what SOP did you create?"

Steps at Algoscale:

  1. Service pages and case studies first, so there was specific content for AI engines to cite.
  2. PR placements next to the Tier-1 firms buyers already compare against.
  3. Community distribution on Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn and niche data and AI forums, run as a separate channel.
  4. Tracked AI Overview placements, citations, and the share of pipeline from AI referrals.

What didn't work: programmatic location pages ("[service] in [location]") and tool pages ("[service] for [tool]"). They didn't prove effective, so I moved the effort to detailed service pages, PR and community distribution.

Done more than once: I ran the same community-distribution programme at Taggbox/Tagshop (Reddit, Quora and forums across four products: 16K → 60K sessions, 50+ enterprise MQLs a month) and then again at Algoscale.

The SOP format: Task 4 of my SoluLab assignment, for getting onto the third-party sources AI engines already cite. It sets out steps, inputs, a QC checklist, fortnightly measurement, and rules for repeating, improving or stopping. I tested it on SoluLab: 45 dated runs showed that SoluLab's only AI recommendation came through two third-party lists.

Q3. Lead generation and commercial impact (3 min)

"Give us one example where your LLM visibility or content distribution work generated qualified B2B opportunities… and how would you adapt it to reach $30K in Q1 and $250K annually?"

Evidence:

  • Algoscale: $1M+ pipeline in 6 months and $450K revenue realised through organic and PR alone. More than 10% of monthly pipeline from AI referrals. US and Western leads +25% month on month.
  • Insighto.ai: 50-60 MQLs a month through a product-led motion, and $100K ARR.
  • Taggbox: 50+ qualified enterprise MQLs a month from content-led demand generation.

Adapting it for SoluLab:

  • Attribution in week 1:
    • a GA4 AI-referrer channel
    • first-touch source passed into HubSpot (already on the site)
    • a required "How did you hear about us?" field with an AI-assistant option
    • deals tagged "AI-influenced"
  • Deal math: SoluLab's typical Clutch project size is $50K-$199K. So $250K is about 3-5 deals and $30K is one first-phase deal. That needs about 15-20 AI-influenced SQLs a year at a 20-25% close rate.
  • Q1 order:
    1. Fix entity consistency, which protects deals already in the pipeline.
    2. Get onto the lists cited for vendor-selection prompts. SoluLab is on 3 of the 15 most-cited lists against LeewayHertz's 10.
    3. Build an answer-first cost hub for buyers who are budgeting.
  • Reported weekly: AI-influenced SQLs and pipeline against the $30K and $250K targets.
