Questions

Questions about AI we get asked

What does AI consulting with Qubik involve?

A free AI readiness call, then a scoping stage where we map your processes and systems and show you a working prototype, then the build, then ongoing support. You'll know the price before any work starts.

We're not sure we're ready for AI. Is that a problem?

No. Most businesses we talk to aren't sure. The readiness call and scoping tell you what's ready now, what needs fixing first, and whether AI is worth it for you at all.

What can AI actually do in our business?

The most common uses are reading documents and entering the details, answering routine customer enquiries, drafting reports and summaries, flagging unusual trends and forecasting from booked work. We'll tell you which would pay back first.

Is our data safe with AI?

Yes. We only use the access the work needs, read-only wherever possible. Dashboards and tools sit behind secure sign-in, and access can be limited by role. Your people approve anything that matters.

Will AI replace our staff?

It takes the repetitive part of their work, not the people. Most businesses use the time it frees up to grow without hiring more admin, and to give good people more interesting work.

Do you work with businesses in Sydney, Melbourne, Perth and Adelaide?

Yes. We're based in Brisbane and work with businesses across Australia, by video and in person where it helps.

Will it work with our software?

We can normally work with any software you have: accounting, payroll, CRM, operational systems and spreadsheets. We'll check your setup on the call.

How long does it take?

We move as quickly as your team can handle. You'll see a working prototype early in scoping, which typically takes two to three weeks, and you'll have a timeline before you commit to anything.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you want AI to do and which systems are involved, so we don't publish packages. See how pricing works above; you'll always know the price before any work starts.
