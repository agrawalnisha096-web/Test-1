## 1. Why this list, and why now

These are people who have already shown interest in Qubik. Bringing them back costs mostly time and a small retargeting budget, not new-audience ad spend. The timing helps too. Individual tax season has just closed, and owners are now thinking about cash flow, next year's tax position, and whether their accountant is actually helping them. That's the opening for a "let's look at your year ahead" conversation, well before the EOFY rush.

## 2. Next steps

1. **Export and audit (days 1–3).**
   - Pull the full list with source, date captured, last contact, consent, and whether each contact is a business or an individual.
   - Remove duplicates, bounced and invalid emails, and current clients.
   - Check mobile numbers against the Do Not Call Register before any calls.
2. **Segment (days 3–5).**
   - **A: businesses, enquired or lapsed in the last 2 years.** Dominic or a senior accountant calls them personally.
   - **B: older business enquiries.** Email and SMS, plus Meta retargeting.
   - **C: individuals, very old records, or no record of consent.** Meta custom audience only, or excluded.
3. **Consent check.**
   - Email and SMS go only where consent was given or can reasonably be inferred from a past enquiry or client relationship with Qubik.
   - Every message identifies Qubik and carries a working unsubscribe, actioned within 5 working days (ACMA).
   - Anyone uncertain goes into the Meta audience rather than the inbox, once the privacy policy covers it.
   - *Confirm this with Qubik's privacy adviser.*
4. **Offers to test** (one per wave, so we learn which works):
   - **Business owners:** a free 30-minute "year-ahead" review covering tax position, cash flow and the structure for 2026–27, leading to Business Basics or Business Advisory Solutions.
   - **"Is your accountant helping you grow?":** a free second opinion on last year's return or BAS for owners thinking of switching.
   - **Optional, A$3M+ businesses only:** an introduction to Qubik's new AI & Automation service. This is tagged separately so it doesn't blur the accounting results.
5. **The message.** A short note from Dominic carrying the reel: "It's been a while since we spoke. Here's a 60-second look at what we're doing for businesses like yours [reel]. Want a free 30-minute look at your year ahead?" People can reply, use the booking link, or take a call from the team.
6. **Send in waves.**
   - **Wave 1** (days 8–14): about 600 contacts from segment A and the best of B.
   - **Waves 2–3** (days 15–30): the rest of the contactable list, using whatever worked in wave 1.
7. **Retargeting.** Upload all contactable leads to Meta as a custom audience (hashed). Show them the reel for 30 days, then a second, offer-led ad to anyone who watched at least half of it.
8. **Follow-up and the EOFY wave.**
   - Two follow-ups over 10 days.
   - Everyone else gets a useful monthly email: a tax tip, a deadline reminder, a short client story.
   - A second, bigger wave goes out in **April–May** for EOFY tax planning, the peak buying window for accounting.

## 3. Thresholds per wave

| Signal | Keep going | Change something | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reply or booking rate | 2%+ | 1–2%: rewrite the message or switch offer | Under 1% |
| Unsubscribes and complaints | Under 1% | — | Over 1%: stop and review the list and message |
| Email bounces | Under 3% | 3–5%: clean the list | Over 5%, or any spam block: stop email and fix the sending domain |

Send from a **separate sending domain**, so qubik.com.au's email reputation is protected while old addresses are tested.

| Step | Conservative | Base | Assumption |

| Qualified (a fit for Qubik's services) | 18 | 32 | 40% |
| Meetings held | 13 | 22 | 70% show up |
| New clients | 4 | 9 | 30–40% of meetings; warm leads close better than cold |
| New annual fees | 4 × ⟦average annual fee⟧ | 9 × ⟦average annual fee⟧ | Illustration: at A$4,000 a year, A$16,000–36,000 of recurring fees |

Accounting fees recur, so each new client keeps paying in later years. The value of the list is therefore larger than the first-year numbers suggest.
