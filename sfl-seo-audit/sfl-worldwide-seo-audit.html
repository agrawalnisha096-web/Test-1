01Summary

sflworldwide.com passes the basic on-page checks: all 455 sitemap pages return 200, every page has a meta description and one H1, and no image lacks alt text. The problems are elsewhere:

  • Search visibility. SFL appears for 10 of 40 core non-brand queries, 8 of them about India. It does not appear for any of 12 other destination countries or for any general service query.
  • Lost pages. 144 URLs listed in the site's own llms.txt return 404 or 410, with no redirect. One of them still ranks #3.
  • Hidden pages. 61 sitemap pages can't be reached by following links from the homepage. A further 146 blog posts are 4 or more clicks deep.
  • Canonical errors. Five service pages declare the homepage as their canonical URL.
  • Content quality. Country pages share a median 32% of their text with 10 or more sibling pages. Blog posts carry 8.1 figures per 1,000 words, against 10.7–55.8 on competitor blogs.
  • Structured data. No blog post carries any structured data.
  • Page weight. Median HTML is 784 KB, the heaviest of the 12 sites measured.

04Technical audit

4.1 Indexing and canonical tags

CriticalFive service pages canonicalised to the homepage

/carriers/shipping-with-fedex, /carriers/shipping-with-dhl, /carriers/shipping-with-ups, /carriers/shipping-with-usps and /services/domestic-shipping each have 1,255–2,629 words of their own content. Each declares <link rel="canonical" href="https://www.sflworldwide.com">, and its hreflang set points to the homepage and its locale versions. Domestic Shipping is linked from 382 of 383 US pages.

Fix: give each page a self-referencing canonical, and hreflang tags that point to its own locale versions or none.

MediumNoindex page in the sitemap and a 404 template with conflicting tags

  • /shipping-moving-to-india-from-the-usa is noindex, nofollow but listed in the sitemap.
  • The 404 template sends status 404 with two robots tags, noindex and index, follow, and uses the homepage title.

Fix: remove the page from the sitemap; give the 404 template a single noindex and a "Page not found" title.

4.2 Architecture and internal links

CriticalPages that links don't reach

  • The deepest reachable post is 51 clicks from the homepage.
  • The homepage links to 42 internal URLs, all of them site-wide navigation.
  • /blog lists 9 posts behind a "Load More" button, and /blog?page=2 returns the same 9 posts.
  • The HTML sitemap page lists no blog posts.
  • robots.txt disallows /blog/page/.
  • 12 sitemap pages receive no internal links from any other page.

Fix: add paginated archive URLs linked with <a href> and topic hubs (India, Car shipping, Moving, Customs, Packing). Link country pages to their guides and posts, and each post back to its country and service pages. Remove the /blog/page/ disallow rule.

MediumAnchor text and non-crawlable links

69 internal links use "READ MORE" as their anchor text. The crawl found 275 href="#" links. Lighthouse flagged <a> elements without an href on blog posts.

4.3 Retired URLs and broken links

Critical144 retired URLs with no redirect

/llms.txt lists 509 URLs: 363 return 200, 136 return 410, 8 return 404 and 2 redirect. Retired URLs that still appear in search results:

  • /blog/is-it-worth-shipping-tv-from-usa-to-india: #3 for "how to ship a tv from usa to india".
  • /blog/international-priority-shipping-with-fedex.
  • /blog/seamless-shipping-from-usa-to-uk-your-ultimate-guide-with-sfl-worldwide.
  • /worldwide-destinations/shipping-to-uk/electronics-iteams.

Live pages link to 34 dead URLs, 58 links in all. Eight of those links are on the HTML sitemap page. The author pages /author/punit-manchanda and /author/maria-hernandez return 200 but are missing from the XML sitemap.

Fix: 301-redirect each retired URL to its closest live page; update the 58 links; rebuild llms.txt from the live sitemap; add the author pages to the sitemap.

4.4 XML sitemap and robots.txt

MediumSitemap signals

  • 435 of 455 lastmod values are 2026-09-25, the same date.
  • The sitemap includes a noindex page, the 5 canonicalised pages and the empty /en-in/blog.
  • robots.txt keeps WordPress-era rules (/wp-json/, /wp-admin/admin-ajax.php, ?page_id=, /blog/tag/).
  • The Cloudflare Content-Signal comment block has no Content-Signal: directive.

Fix: use each page's real modification date for lastmod, and list only indexable, self-canonical pages.

4.5 International sections

MediumLocale copies and hreflang gaps

  • /en-in/ has 43 pages and /en-ca/ has 29. 31 of those 72 pages have no hreflang.
  • 12 page pairs across the three locales have identical text, and 26 pairs share 83% or more.
  • /en-in/blog shows "No blog posts available yet".

Fix: keep locale pages that have local content (prices, offices, phone numbers); point pure copies' canonicals at the US page or remove them; complete hreflang on the rest.

4.6 Structured data and social tags

HighStructured data gaps

  • 202 of 455 pages carry JSON-LD. None of the 246 blog posts does.
  • The 9 Organization blocks have no sameAs.
  • There is no LocalBusiness markup for the 5 offices and no WebSite markup.
  • One FAQ answer is empty: "What is the approximate cost of shipping from the USA to Australia?" on /worldwide-destinations/shipping-to-australia.

MediumShare image

438 of 455 pages use /assets/uploads/2023/08/home-ship.png as og:image. That image is 211×153 px; Facebook's minimum is 200×200 and its recommended size is 1,200×630. Two posts have no og:image.

HighWhat adds the weight

  • HTML. The median document is 784 KB and the homepage 852 KB, of which 443 KB on the homepage is the inline Next.js data payload. Readable text is 1.2% of the HTML.
  • No HTML caching. Pages are sent with private, no-cache, no-store, and Cloudflare reports DYNAMIC (not cached).
  • Third-party scripts. Google Tag Manager (522 KB), the Facebook pixel and Microsoft Clarity block the main thread for 1.2–1.7 s.
  • Images. The author photos /author/Punit.png (612 KB) and /author/marianew.png (754 KB) are 1,200×1,200 PNGs loaded at full size on every post. The /en-in LCP image is lazy-loaded.
  • Console errors. The homepage logs React hydration error #418, and every page logs a CSP error for a blocked Facebook frame.

Fix: cut and delay the tag-manager tags; cache HTML at the edge; serve images through next/image at display size; load the LCP image with priority; fix the hydration mismatch.

05Content quality

5.1 Measures

All 55 pages were measured on their main content, with navigation, header, footer and forms removed:

  • Word count.
  • Readability: Flesch Reading Ease.
  • Specificity: figures per 1,000 words, counting prices, days, weights and percentages.
  • Freshness: mentions of 2026.
  • Structure: tables and lists.
  • Filler: stock sales phrases per 1,000 words, such as "seamless", "hassle-free" and "your trusted partner".
  • Citations: links to government and customs sites.
  • Coverage: the share of a topic checklist the page covers, for example de minimis threshold, transit days, price per lb or kg, KYC documents and battery rules.
