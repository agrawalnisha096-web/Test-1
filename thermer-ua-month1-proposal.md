**Commitment:** 6–7 hours a week (about 10–12 in week 1 for setup), mostly weekdays, with quick checks on other days after any change. Actual vs. planned hours go in every weekly report. ## How I'll follow the SOP

Everything non-negotiable stays as written: no optimising for installs alone, no budget increase or new campaign without written approval, 10–20% steps only, every change logged and checked within 24 hours, and immediate escalation of spend or tracking issues. To fit the hours, a few items are lighter in month 1:

| SOP item | Month 1 |
|---|---|

| Documentation | Concise versions of the Confluence templates and a one-page weekly report |
| D30 retention, revenue growth | Baseline only; targets agreed for months 2–3, once cohorts mature |
