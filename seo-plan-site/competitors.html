Cast AI wins on volume, not quality

10,315 US keywords, but 85% sit on pages 6–10. Its biggest traffic page is a Google Cloud pricing post, not a buyer query. The real gap is links (3,735 domains) and proof (42 case studies).

ScaleOps is beatable in search

Only 232 ranking keywords and 161 indexed URLs. Its blog has slowed to 1–4 posts a month since July, and it blocks GPTBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended, so AI answers can't read it. It wins on LinkedIn spend.

DevZero ranks, but for the wrong queries

458 top-10 keywords, almost all cloud pricing and instance pages. Not in the top 100 for "kubernetes cost optimization", and no Google ads.

Nautili's rivals own the brand searches

LiteLLM gets 27,100 searches a month, Portkey 5,400, Vercel AI Gateway 2,900. Nautili has compare pages for only Portkey and OpenRouter.

Fastest wins

  • DevZero: own "rightsizing without restarts". No vendor ranks on page 1 for it today, and it's DevZero's headline claim
  • Nautili: own the AI-coding-cost queries: "claude code pricing" (33,100 a month, $3.09 a click) and "cursor pricing" (9,900, $10.68). Weak competition
  • Both: AI answers. DevZero and Nautili allow AI crawlers; ScaleOps blocks them and Cast AI's llms.txt is thin

Cast AI's link lead is the real gap.

Cast AI

WordPress + Yoast on Cloudflare. Fast (0.10 s), rich schema, a 2,168-word server-rendered homepage, and the heaviest tracking stack of the four: Google, LinkedIn, Meta, Reddit, Bing, X, Vector and Qualified.

What they do well

Strong

Content volume and breadth

317 posts across Karpenter, GPU, spot, FinOps, LLM cost and troubleshooting. A Sept 2026 burst refreshed 168 posts in one month.

Strong

Bottom-of-funnel pages

Cast AI vs ScaleOps and vs PerfectScale pages, 'best kubecost alternatives' and similar posts, and their own '8 Best Kubernetes Cost Optimization Tools' listicle with ItemList + FAQ schema.

Strong

Proof and link magnets

42 case studies, annual reports, a spot availability map (#2 for 'spot availability'), a glossary, analyst pages and 86 press releases. This is why they have 3,735 referring domains.

Strong

Owns the head term

#1 for 'kubernetes cost optimization' with a dedicated landing page; #3 for 'kubernetes cost optimization tools'.

Weaknesses DevZero can use

Medium

Most rankings are weak or off-topic

8,726 of 10,315 keywords rank 51–100. Off-topic posts (Active Directory, AWS Glue, IAM) dilute focus.

Medium

Indexing housekeeping

/alternative-to/ is in the sitemap but redirects to the homepage; an ad URL with campaign parameters ranks for 'what is cast ai'.

Medium

Weak llms.txt

A short generic list. It allows AI crawlers but gives them little to quote.

Gap

No page owns 'without restarts'

'pod rightsizing without restart' returns no vendor page in the top 9. DevZero's headline claim is open.

ScaleOps

WordPress on Cloudflare with HubSpot, Chili Piper and Drift. A paid-and-sales-led company: its SEO footprint is small and shrinking (102 new keywords vs 152 lost in the latest period).

Strong

#1 for two valuable category queries

'kubernetes cost optimization tools' and 'karpenter vs cluster autoscaler'. Also top 5 for 'resource limits', 'k8s hpa', 'vllm kubernetes'.

Strong

Clean product architecture

23 product pages, one per use case (rightsizing, Karpenter, spot, GPU, Java, air-gapped, MCP), each targeting a keyword cluster. Good llms.txt.

Critical

Blocks AI search engines

robots.txt disallows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended and CCBot. AI answers can't draw on ScaleOps' own pages. DevZero allows them: a real edge.

High

Slow and image-heavy

Homepage TTFB 1.51 s; 149 of 156 homepage images have no alt text.

Medium

Sloppy indexing

A typography test page, /book-a-demo-2-2/ and a broken template URL sit in the sitemap. /compare/ and /pricing/ have no H1.

Medium

Content has slowed

88 posts in total, 1–4 a month since July 2026. One compare hub and two 'vs' posts.

Where Nautili can stand apart

Every rival claims routing, failover and "one API". The open space is spend control: who spent what, budgets, and routing to cheaper models, which Nautili already sells (4% fee vs about 5.5% on OpenRouter, bring your own keys, self-host). Use developer words ("LLM cost", "cost per developer"). Avoid "AI spend management": Google shows finance software (1Password, Zylo, Concur) for it, at $60 a click.

What clicks cost

Search demand sits in tool names: "cast ai" 1,900/mo, "scaleops" 1,300, "kubecost" 880, "sedai" 720, versus 90 for "kubernetes cost optimization". Bidding on competitor names costs about $10–26 a click. On the Nautili side, "claude code cost" is $3.61 and "claude code pricing" $3.09.
