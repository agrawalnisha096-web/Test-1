Tier 1 · the top 10

Goal: Google top 10 for each

  • One page per keyword, no two pages competing for the same term. All 10 already have a page to build on; the work is refreshing them, plus alternatives pages for Cast AI, ScaleOps and Kubecost
  • Each page: the keyword in title and H1, a direct answer in the first 100 words, an FAQ block, schema, and a 'check your cluster' CTA into analyze-cluster
  • Internal links: 5+ links into each page from related posts, compare pages and the 5,200 instance pages. Fix the pages that compete with each other first (AKS pricing, EKS, GKE, Karpenter, VPA): one intent per URL
  • Weeks 1–2: AKS/GKE/EKS title rewrites (already ranking 5–8) and the cost pillar. Weeks 2–6: alternatives pages and autoscaler refreshes
  • Links and mentions: pitch the 20 'best tools' listicles, answer Reddit threads on Cast AI and VPA restarts
  • Track weekly in Search Console. Anything stuck at 8–20 after 8 weeks gets a refresh and 3 more internal links
Tier 2 · keywords 11–50

Goal: top 50 for all, then top 20

  • Covered by the content calendar at 8 pieces a month: each post targets one keyword and links up to its category's top-10 page
  • Refresh first: posts already ranking 8–30 move fastest (Karpenter vs CA, GPU utilization, CRIU, pricing pages). Strong guides that barely rank (KEDA, Karpenter on EKS) need links, not rewrites
  • The pricing posts hold 889k impressions at 0.25% CTR: link the orphaned ones and add calculator call-outs
  • Competitor names (Sedai, OpenCost, PerfectScale, Spot.io) each get a compare page; pricing queries become sections, not new pages
  • Broad terms (finops, vllm) are top-50 targets only. They bring awareness, not demos
  • Month 3 goal: every keyword in the top 50. Month 6 goal: half of them in the top 20
