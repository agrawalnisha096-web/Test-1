How to run it

  • Weeks 1–2: 50% budget, Maximize Clicks with suggested CPC caps: $30 competitors, $18 autoscaling, $35 cost, $15 rightsizing and GPU (the 50% start is from the growth plan)
  • Week 3: full budget; clone the converting ad groups to EU and India
  • Competitor ads: bid on the brand names, but don't put Cast AI, ScaleOps or Kubecost in ad text. Trademark complaints get ads disapproved
  • Small campaigns: Rightsizing and GPU only have ~500–700 US searches a month, so they may not spend their full budget. Move what's left to Competitor alternatives, but keep them running: they're the highest-intent terms
  • Report on: cost per booked demo and per analyze-cluster signup. Move to Maximize Conversions at ~30 conversions a month
