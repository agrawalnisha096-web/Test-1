Where Nautili can stand apart

Every rival claims routing, failover and "one API". The open space is spend control: who spent what, budgets, and routing to cheaper models, which Nautili already sells (4% fee vs about 5.5% on OpenRouter, bring your own keys, self-host). Use developer words ("LLM cost", "cost per developer"). Avoid "AI spend management": Google shows finance software (1Password, Zylo, Concur) for it, at $60 a click.
