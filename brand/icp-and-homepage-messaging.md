# Mosta Group: who we sell to and what the homepage must do (27 Nov 2026)

**Basis and confidence:** built from what we know so far: Qubik's client base (established businesses, 10 to 1,000+ staff), the Google Ads and Clarity data (generic "AI agency" searchers did not convert), and Keyword Planner. No buyer interviews yet. **Medium confidence.** Validate with Dominic: list the 5 clients he would most like 10 more of, and the exact words they used when they first asked for help.

## The buyer in one line
An established business (about 20 to 500 staff) whose growth now runs through manual work, led by someone who is too busy to research AI and does not trust vendors to be neutral.

## Five problems they arrive with (their words, not ours)

| # | Who | What they say | What is really going on | Trigger that makes them search |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Owner or CEO | "Every time sales grow, we hire another person to push paper." | Growth runs through re-keying, chasing approvals, hand-built reports | A new hire request, margins squeezed by wages |
| 2 | CFO or finance manager | "By the time the numbers are ready, they're already old." | Bank, Xero and spreadsheets joined by people copying data | Board wants faster numbers, a finance team member leaves |
| 3 | COO, multi-site business | "I can't see what's happening across our sites until it's too late." | Each site has its own spreadsheets and habits | A new site opens, a compliance scare |
| 4 | CEO or board | "Everyone says we need AI. I don't know where to start, and I won't waste money finding out." | Advice comes from vendors selling one tool; staff already use AI with no rules | Board asks for an AI plan, a competitor announces one |
| 5 | Owner or finance lead | "If one person left tomorrow, nobody would know how this works." | Critical processes live in fragile spreadsheets and one person's head | Key person resigns or makes a costly error |

## What stops them contacting us
- "It'll cost a fortune and take months." → Free review first; a working pilot on one process before any bigger commitment.
- "Will it work with our systems?" → Built on what they already use (Qubik: Xero partner since 2013; confirm the others we support).
- "Is our data safe?" → Plain answer on where data goes and who can see it.
- "My team will resist it." → We automate the parts people hate, and train the team.
- "Who maintains it?" → Support plan after go-live.
- "You're an accounting firm, not an AI company." → Turn it round: business advisers first; we start from the business case, not a tool.

## What the homepage must do
1. **First 5 seconds:** the visitor sees their own problem, in their words. Hero = "which of these sounds like you?" with the five problems.
2. **Next 30 seconds:** they believe we can fix it: what we would change (before → after), who we are (since 2007), one real result (needed from Dominic).
3. **One easy action:** a free 30-minute review, offered three ways: book a call, a three-field form ("the job you'd like gone"), or the phone number.
4. **Answer the objections** above on the page, briefly.
5. **Partners** (accounting firms, IT providers) get their own page, linked from the menu and footer, not the hero.
