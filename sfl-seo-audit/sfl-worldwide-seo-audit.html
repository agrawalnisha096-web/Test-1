Technical, content and competitor audit

SFL Worldwide SEO Audit

sflworldwide.com has a clean technical base, but it now ranks for little outside India. This audit measures why, on the same scale for SFL and the sites that outrank it, and ranks the fixes.

01Summary

SFL is not failing on basic SEO hygiene. Titles, descriptions, H1s, canonicals and alt text are as good as or better than the competitors'. It is failing on four things that matter more:

  1. Lost and hidden pages. 144 URLs that SFL itself still lists now return 404 or 410. Some of them still rank, and none redirect. Another 49 live pages can't be reached by following links from the homepage, and 149 more blog posts are four or more clicks deep.
  2. Content that doesn't answer the query. SFL's pages are long but thin on facts. The blog carries 8.2 numbers per 1,000 words; competitor blogs carry 13 to 56. Rates are dated "November 2023". A 1,657-word post titled "How Long Does It Take to Ship from the USA to India?" never states a number of days.
  3. Templated pages at scale. About 32% of each of the 51 country pages repeats across ten or more sibling pages. Sales filler is several times denser than on competitor pages.
  4. Missing machine-readable signals and heavy pages. None of the 246 blog posts has Article markup; most competitors mark up 89–100%. SFL's median HTML is 779 KB, the heaviest of the 12 sites measured. Mobile LCP is 5.2–8.7 s on 5 of 6 pages tested.

The result in search: SFL appeared for 11 of 40 non-brand queries, and 10 of those were about India. It did not appear for any of its other 12 main country pages, or for any general service query.

05Technical audit

5.1 Indexing and canonical tags

CriticalFive service pages point their canonical at the homepage

/carriers/shipping-with-fedex, -dhl, -ups, -usps and /services/domestic-shipping each have 1,255–2,629 words of their own. But <link rel="canonical"> and all four hreflang tags point to the homepage. Domestic Shipping is linked from every page on the site.

Fix: make each page's canonical its own URL, and point its hreflang tags at real locale equivalents or remove them. This is most likely a single template fallback bug.

MediumNoindex, 404 and ad landing pages

/shipping-moving-to-india-from-the-usa is noindex, nofollow but listed in the sitemap. The 404 template outputs both noindex and index, follow robots tags and uses the homepage title.

Fix: remove the page from the sitemap. Make the 404 template output a single noindex and a "Page not found" title.

5.2 Architecture, click depth and internal links

CriticalMany pages are hard or impossible to reach through links

Clicks needed to reach each sitemap page from the homepage, counting only links a crawler can follow:

The deepest blog posts are reachable only through a chain of related-post links, up to 51 clicks long. The causes:

  • The blog index shows 9 posts. The rest load through a JavaScript "Load More" button, and /blog?page=2 returns the same 9 posts.
  • The HTML sitemap page links to 0 posts.
  • The homepage links to only the 41 URLs in the site-wide navigation.
  • robots.txt still disallows /blog/page/, the old WordPress pagination path.

Fix: add crawlable, paginated blog archives and topic hubs (India, Car shipping, Moving, Customs, Packing). Link each country page to its guides and posts, and each post back to its country and service pages. Remove the conflicting disallow rule.

MediumWeak link signals

  • "READ MORE" is the anchor text on 72 internal links.
  • There are 269 href="#" links, and several <a> elements on blog posts have no href, so crawlers can't follow them.
  • Nine sitemap pages have no internal links from any other sitemap page.

5.3 Removed content and broken links

Critical144 retired URLs, none redirected, some still ranking

  • Of the 509 URLs in /llms.txt, 129 return 410 and 15 return 404. Several are 2026 posts, such as "How Much to Ship a Car to Texas: Cost & Tips 2026".
  • At least 9 dead SFL URLs still appear in search results. Two hold non-brand rankings:
    • /blog/is-it-worth-shipping-tv-from-usa-to-india at #3.
    • /blog/international-car-shipping-from-usa at #5.
  • 41 dead URLs are still linked from 69 places on live pages, including 11 links on the HTML sitemap page.

Fix: pause further deletions. Redirect each dead URL that has traffic or backlinks to its closest live equivalent. Update the 69 internal links, and regenerate llms.txt from the live sitemap.

MediumExternal links

108 distinct external URLs. 72 return 200. One is confirmed dead: chinahighlights.com/festivals/china-public-holiday.htm returns 404. Most of the rest refuse automated checks (403/429) and are probably fine. Three accreditation sites (ARA, IMA, MobilityEx) couldn't be reached from this environment, and each is linked from 340 pages, so check them by hand.

5.4 Sitemap and robots

MediumThe sitemap gives no useful freshness signal

435 of 455 lastmod values share one build timestamp, 96% of entries and the second-worst share of the 12 sites. The best sites vary by page: WCS 4% and Schumacher 5%. The sitemap also:

  • lists a noindex page, the five canonicalised pages and an empty /en-in/blog;
  • leaves out the two author pages, which are indexable and linked more than 300 times each.

robots.txt keeps old WordPress rules, and its Cloudflare "Content-Signal" comment block has no actual directive.

5.5 International sections

MediumCopies instead of localised pages

  • 12 page pairs across /, /en-in/ and /en-ca/ are 100% identical, and 26 pairs are 83% or more similar.
  • 31 of the 72 locale pages have no hreflang.
  • /en-in/blog says "No blog posts available yet" but is listed in the sitemap.

Competitors either localise fully (Parcel Monkey, Meest and Seven Seas have hreflang on 100% of sampled pages) or don't run locale copies at all.

Fix: keep the locale pages that have real local content (prices in INR or CAD, local offices). Point pure copies' canonicals at the US version or remove them, and complete hreflang on the rest.

5.6 Structured data and social tags

HighThe blog has no machine-readable article or author data

  • 0 of 246 posts carry Article/BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList or Person markup, even though each shows an author name and a date.
  • Shippo, Meest and Qwintry mark up 100% of sampled posts, Easyship 96% and WCS 89%.
  • Only 44% of SFL's 455 pages (35% of the random sample) carry any JSON-LD, against 88–100% of sampled pages for 9 of the 11 competitors.
  • Other gaps: the Organization markup has no sameAs (18 instances), there's no LocalBusiness for the 5 offices and no WebSite, and two FAQ answers are empty.

MediumShare image too small and identical everywhere

438 of 455 pages use /assets/uploads/2023/08/home-ship.png as og:image. It is 211×153 px, below Facebook's 200×200 px minimum (the recommended size is 1,200×630). Two new posts have no share image at all.

HighWhat makes the pages slow

  • HTML weight. The median document is 779 KB. On the homepage, 443 KB of that is the inline Next.js data payload. Only 1.2% of the HTML is readable text, against 5–13% for Easyship, Shippo, Schumacher, WCS, Qwintry and ShipIndus.
  • No HTML caching. Pages are sent with private, no-cache, no-store, so Cloudflare never caches them. JavaScript files are marked private too, which keeps them out of shared caches. Parcel Monkey, Jio and WCS cache HTML at the edge.
  • Third-party scripts. Google Tag Manager (about 522 KB), the Facebook pixel and Microsoft Clarity together block the main thread for 1.2–1.7 s.
  • Images. Author photos are 1,200×1,200 PNGs (612 KB and 754 KB) loaded at full size on all 246 posts, without image optimisation. The /en-in hero image is lazy-loaded, which delays LCP.
  • Script errors. The homepage throws React hydration error #418. Every page logs a CSP error for a blocked Facebook frame.

06Content quality audit

6.1 How content was scored

55 pages that compete for the same queries were grouped into 13 topics. Each was measured on its main content, with navigation, header, footer and forms removed:

  • Depth: word count.
  • Readability: Flesch Reading Ease, where 50–60 reads as plain English.
  • Specificity: figures and prices per 1,000 words.
  • Freshness: mentions of 2026, mentions of 2024 or earlier, and a visible date.
  • Structure: tables and lists.
  • Evidence: links to official sources (government and customs sites), with accreditation badges and social links excluded.
  • Filler: sales phrases such as "seamless", "hassle-free" and "your trusted partner" per 1,000 words.
  • Coverage: the share of a topic checklist the page covers. The checklist lists the subtopics searchers need, such as de minimis thresholds, transit days, prices per lb or kg, KYC documents and 2026 rule changes.

Scores for every page are in data/content-quality-scores.csv.

What the table shows. SFL's pages are usually longer than the competition. They lose on specificity, freshness and focus: fewer figures per word, no 2026 references, and two to seven times more sales filler. Where SFL wrote a data-dense page (cost per kg, electronics), it matches or beats the competition, and the cost-per-kg post is one of the few that still ranks.

6.3 Posts that don't answer their own title

All 246 posts were checked: posts titled with a time question should state days or weeks, and posts titled with a cost question should state a price.

  • 1 of 9 transit-time posts gives no number. "How Long Does It Take to Ship from the USA to India?" runs 1,657 words and never states a day count. It was published 18 July 2023 and never updated.
  • 9 of 41 cost posts give no price, including:
    • "How Much Does International Relocation Cost";
    • "All You Need to Know About Our International Courier Charges";
    • "Send Laptop from USA to India: Costs, Process, Tips";
    • "Shipping a Car to New Zealand: Costs & Process", published in September 2026.

Searchers who land on these pages go back to the results and pick a competitor that gives the number. The full list is in the evidence files.

6.4 Freshness

  • The Pakistan and Nigeria pages state: "The discounted rates mentioned below were verified and updated in November 2023."
  • The India page tells readers to "see the latest 2025 prices".
  • None of the 8 SFL country and service pages scored mentions 2026. Winning competitor guides are built around 2026 rates and rule changes: the UK £135 VAT threshold, Germany ending its low-value duty exemption on 1 July 2026, China's tariffs, and the 2026 carrier rate increases.
  • On all 246 posts, "Last updated" equals "Published on", so the site never shows that a post was revised.
  • Only 6% of posts mention 2026. On competitor blogs the figure is 46–100%.

6.5 Templated country pages

After each page's country name is replaced with a placeholder, a median of 32% of a country page's text appears on 10 or more other SFL country pages (range 11–38%). The most alike pairs:

  • New Zealand and Saudi Arabia: 68%.
  • Colombia and New Zealand: 58%.
  • Haiti and Jamaica: 56%.
  • Denmark and Finland: 54%.

The same H2s repeat across the set: "Get an Instant Quote" (51 pages), "Looking X? Explore Here!" (51), "Why use SFL Worldwide?" (49). 67 page titles use "cheapest", "cheap" or "affordable". SFL's text is original compared with competitors: it shares about 1% with Jio Worldwide and about 0.1% with Meest. The duplication is internal. Google's spam policies describe "scaled content abuse" and favour pages with substantial unique value. In the searches checked, only the India pages from this set rank at all.

07Why competitors win

Easyship

14 queries · 10 in top 3 · Tranco 124k

A calculator page for every route, plus data-heavy guides: a median 3,593 words, tables in 61% of posts, and 100% Article markup and 2026 mentions. Its HTML is a third the size of SFL's.

Parcel Monkey

13 queries · 8 in top 3 · Tranco 498k

Short country pages (about 800 words) that lead with "from just $26.82" and a calculator. Product, FAQ and WebSite markup on every page, full hreflang, and HTML cached at the edge.

Meest USA

9 queries · 8 in top 3 · Tranco 178k

A specialist for diaspora shipping routes. The meta description carries "$35 / 5 lb", insurance and customs limits. 100% schema coverage and light HTML (183 KB).

Shippo

7 queries · 5 in top 3 · Tranco 56k

2026 guides organised around what changed: VAT thresholds, new duty rules, live carrier rates. Up to 7 tables per guide, BlogPosting and Person markup, and 92 KB HTML.

Jio Worldwide

9 queries · positions 4–7 · not in top 1M

SFL's own model and tagline ("Save UPTO 70%"), with price-led titles ("starts at just $29.99"), visible Google review stars and dates, and separate pages for single items. Its text isn't copied from SFL.

WCS and Schumacher Cargo

Car shipping · 3–5 queries each, top 3

Instant car-shipping calculators, 83–92% topic coverage (RoRo, ports, title, duties), and Schumacher's Product markup with a 4.7 rating from 6,445 reviews. SFL no longer has an international car page.

The winners share three habits. They put a concrete number in the search result. They keep the data current to 2026. And each page does one job, whether as a calculator, a data guide or a single route. None of them wins on length.
