Messy GraphQL is hard to read and easy to get wrong. Here's how to fix it in
seconds with the [GraphQL Formatter](https://jsonformatterpro.com/graphql-formatter/).

## Paste your query

Drop any query, mutation, or schema into the **Input** box.

## Click Format

The formatter indents fields and nests selection sets so the structure is
obvious. Prefer the smallest output? Click **Minify** instead.

## Copy or download

Use **Copy Output** to grab the result, or **Download .graphql** to save it.

> Everything runs in your browser — your GraphQL never leaves your machine.
