# Crown — Digital Marketing Agency Portfolio (Editable)

**`Crown_Portfolio.pptx`** is a fully editable, professionally designed agency
portfolio, adapted from the original `Aakash_Pareek_Portfolio_updated.pdf`.

## What it is

An agency-branded, 37-slide 16:9 PowerPoint deck for **Crown**. All of the real
work (analytics, ad previews, campaign screenshots, creative samples,
certifications, images) and every metric are preserved exactly. The identity was
changed from a personal portfolio to the agency **Crown**, and the voice shifted
from first-person singular to plural (I / me / my → we / us / our).

## Design

- **One design system** — a single navy / blue / amber palette, consistent
  Calibri typography, and uniform spacing across all slides.
- **Readable layouts** — left-aligned body copy, framed screenshot cards,
  numbered feature grids, and big stat call-outs.
- **Section dividers** — Social Media Marketing, Paid Ads, SEM, More Channels,
  and Creative.

## Contact

The personal contact block (website / email / phone) has been removed from both
the cover and the closing slide. The closing slide now ends on a call to action.
Add Crown's own contact details when ready.

## How to edit it

Open the `.pptx` in **PowerPoint**, **Google Slides**, or **Canva** and edit any
text, image, or color directly.
