## 1. Project summary

**Trillet (trillet.ai)** is an AI voice-agent platform (ultra-realistic phone/voice agents, sub-2s
latency, 500+ MCP/CRM integrations, HIPAA/TCPA/GDPR/ACMA compliant). It sells on three fronts:
a **white-label platform for agencies** (unlimited sub-accounts, ~$299/mo, $0.09–0.12/min), an
**AI receptionist for SMBs** (from ~$49/mo), and **enterprise voice automation** that resolves calls
end-to-end. Competitors: Retell, Vapi, Synthflow, Bland, PlayAI, Autocalls, AIRA. (Company facts are
reconstructed from search results + the owner's Search Console data — trillet.ai could not be crawled
directly; **verify any product claim against the live site before publishing externally.**)

The work is **for Trillet's growth/marketing team**. The goal of this branch is a **distribution
strategy to get Trillet named and cited where buyers actually decide** — inside AI answers
(ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini/Claude/Google AI Overview) and on the third-party pages that rank in
Google (review sites, Reddit, YouTube, listicles, directories) — rather than only optimizing
trillet.ai itself. This started as a "parasite SEO / social distribution" brief and, after a live
DataForSEO data pass, became a combined **SEO + GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** strategy.

## 3. Key findings and decisions (with the numbers, and the "why")

### The pivotal finding (this reframed the whole strategy)
The original hypothesis (in the HTML playbook) was: *"Trillet is absent everywhere — losing both Google
and AI answers."* **The DataForSEO data partly disproved this.** Reality:
- **Trillet is comparatively STRONG in AI answers.** In the white-label cluster, `trillet.ai` is the
  single **most-cited source domain**, and Trillet is **named in 9 of 16** white-label LLM answers across
  four engines. Gemini + Claude named Trillet in **6 of 8** white-label answers.
- **Trillet is WEAK in Google organic.** Only **~7 listings across 20 keywords** in the top-20; parasite
  hosts (Reddit/YouTube) own **26 / 11 / 27** listings across the three clusters.
- **~83% of all LLM citations go to third-party pages**, not any vendor homepage.

**DECISION #1 — Lead with GEO, treat organic/parasite as a secondary track.** Because the win condition
is "get named/cited in AI answers," and those citations come from third-party pages, the strategy is
**get Trillet onto the third-party pages LLMs and Google already trust** (directories, listicles, PR) —
not optimize trillet.ai. *Rejected:* pouring effort into on-page SEO or into fighting Reddit/YouTube for
organic rank first (parasites are only **2 of 256** LLM citations, so they're a Google-organic-only
problem, lower leverage).

### Per-cluster verdicts (priority order set by the owner: white-label → enterprise → receptionist)
- **White-label / agency — DEFEND & EXTEND (strongest suit).** 9/16 LLM mentions; moderate organic (5
  listings). Keep the lead by matching competitor citation footprint.
- **Enterprise — WHITESPACE.** **0/6** LLM mentions, zero organic listings. *Decision:* don't fight
  Salesforce/Genesys/NICE for "best enterprise platform"; win the **capability sub-angles** — HIPAA/
  healthcare (wide open) and CRM-integration — where citations cluster.
- **Receptionist — WHITESPACE, GEO-critical, most winnable.** 0/6 LLM mentions, but **Google AI Overview
  fires heavily here** and cites receptionist specialists (getaira.io, smith.ai, ringcentral, goodcall,
  heyrosie). Trillet is **already cited in the AI Overview for "ai receptionist for hvac."** Voice-AI
  competitors barely rank here → easier entry, especially via **dental/HVAC vertical** listicles.

### The competitor to copy
**Autocalls (autocalls.ai)** is the most-cited white-label rival in LLMs. Its playbook, reverse-engineered
from its backlinks: **130+ AI-tool directory listings** (free, DIY) + **newswire PR** (LLMs cite syndicated
press releases: usatoday/press-release, digitaljournal/pr, einpresswire, businesswire). This is the
cheapest, highest-volume citation lever. Targets are in `competitor_backlinks_autocalls*.csv`.

### Engine behavior (matters for where to aim GEO effort)
- **ChatGPT (via DataForSEO) is a poor signal:** returned citations on only ~1/10 answers and often named
  irrelevant brands (Resemble AI, Murf, Twilio, Google Duplex). **Weight GEO analysis toward Perplexity +
  Gemini + Claude**, which do live retrieval and cite trillet.ai frequently.
- Google **AI Overview fires on ~all 18 tested keywords** — so AI-Overview optimization is real and worth
  targeting, especially in receptionist.

### Corrected assumption (owner pushed on this — verify, don't assume)
**Medium and LinkedIn Pulse do NOT own these SERPs.** Verified against unfiltered results: vendor blogs,
review sites (G2/Capterra/Trustpilot), Reddit, YouTube, listicles and directories rank; Medium only shows
on narrow long-tails and LinkedIn *articles* essentially don't rank here. Treat Medium/LinkedIn as
distribution/reach + a secondary AI-citation source, **not** as organic-SEO parasites. Substack actually
outranked Medium on the build/how-to long-tail.

### Cost outcome
The audit stayed within the **$1.00 DataForSEO free credit.** Main script run logged **$0.29874**
(`cost_log.csv`); with the extra Gemini/Claude probe + backlinks + placement pulls the total was **≈$0.76**
(see gotcha — those extra calls aren't in `cost_log.csv`).

## 7. Gotchas

- **`cost_log.csv` is incomplete.** It only logs the **main** script run ($0.29874). The Gemini/Claude
  probe, backlinks pulls, and placement-target work were **separate** API calls not captured there —
  true total ≈ **$0.76**. Don't quote $0.30 as the full cost.
- **Egress is environment-specific.** The script needs raw outbound to `api.dataforseo.com`. A default
  claude.ai web session is egress-locked; run it in the **"Web access"** environment (or any host with
  open network). Set `DATAFORSEO_LOGIN` / `DATAFORSEO_PASSWORD` (the API password, not the site login),
  and because the CC shell doesn't persist env vars between Bash calls, **pass them inline on the same
  command line** as the `python3 ...` call. The API password was used in a session transcript and should
  be **rotated** in the DataForSEO dashboard.
- **`raw_dump.json` is gitignored** — not on the branch. Regenerate by re-running the script.
- **LLM sample is small** (~28 answers / 14 prompts) and Gemini/Claude cover **white-label only** —
  treat enterprise/receptionist "0 LLM mentions" as directional, not confirmed, until you run step #2.
- **ChatGPT-via-DataForSEO is a weak citation source** (1/10 citations, irrelevant brands). Don't base
  GEO conclusions on it; use Perplexity/Gemini/Claude.
- **`vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com`** appearing in citations is a Gemini grounding redirect — ignore it.
- **The HTML playbook/brief artifacts (not in repo) predate the data** and overstate Trillet's weakness in
  AI answers. `TRILLET_SEO_GEO_STRATEGY.md` is the corrected, canonical version — prefer it.
- **trillet.ai could not be crawled** from the research env; product facts (pricing, features, compliance)

- **DataForSEO may rename AI-Optimization endpoints/model ids.** They worked as of 2026-09-20:
