Questions we get asked

Do we need new software?

Usually not. We build around Xero and the systems you already use, and only suggest a new tool where it clearly pays for itself.

What do you need from us?

Read-only access to your accounting system, a sample of recent invoices or orders, and about two hours of your team's time across the assessment.

How long does a build take?

Most first builds take six to ten weeks. The assessment gives you a timeline before you commit.

Is our data safe?

Your data stays in your own systems. We only access what the automation needs, and your team approves anything the system isn't sure about.

Do you work outside Queensland?

Yes. We're based in Brisbane and work with businesses across Australia, by video and in person where it helps.
