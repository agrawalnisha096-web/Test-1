The real cost

Your people are your biggest cost. How much of their week goes on spreadsheets?

In most growing businesses, skilled, well-paid people spend hours every week collecting, copying and checking numbers so someone else can make a decision. If any of these sound familiar, your business has outgrown its manual processes.

  • Reports depend on one personWhen they're away, or leave, the numbers stop.
  • Month-end means late nightsOvertime and stress just to pull the figures together, weeks after the month ends.
  • Hiring admin just to keep upEvery new customer or site adds headcount, not just revenue.
  • Good people leaveCopy-and-paste work wears people down, and replacing them is expensive.
  • Systems that don't talk to each otherThe same data typed into three places, and different answers to the same question.
  • No clear view of profitYou can't easily see which sites, customers or projects make money until it's too late to act.

Questions

Questions we get asked

We're not a tech business. Is this for us?

Yes. Automation and dashboards are for businesses that want less manual work and clearer numbers, not for tech companies. You don't need technical staff: we build it, explain it in plain English and support it.

Will it work with our software?

We can normally work with any software you have: accounting, payroll, rostering, CRM, operational systems and spreadsheets. We'll check your setup on the call.

Do we have to change our systems?

No. We connect to your systems with read-only access wherever possible, and your team keeps working in them exactly as they do now.

How long does it take?

We're fast, and we move as quickly as your team can handle. You'll see a working prototype early in scoping, which typically takes two to three weeks, and the build moves at the pace your people can test and adopt it. You'll have a timeline before you commit to anything.

What happens on the call?

A relaxed 30-minute conversation by phone or video with business experts, not salespeople. We look for both business efficiencies and technology efficiencies; where the two meet is usually the richest source of opportunities. You'll leave with an honest view of what's worth doing.

Will automation replace our staff?

It replaces the repetitive part of their work, not the people. Most businesses use the time it frees up to grow without hiring more admin, and to give good people more interesting work.

Is our data safe?

We only use the access the work needs, read-only wherever possible. Dashboards sit behind secure sign-in, and access can be limited so each person sees only what they should, for example a manager seeing only their own site.

Where does AI come in?

Where it genuinely helps: reading documents so nobody re-keys them, highlighting unusual trends, and answering questions about your data in everyday language where supported. Your people stay in control of decisions.

What does it cost?

It depends on your processes and systems, so we don't publish packages. See how pricing works above; you'll always know the price before any work starts.
