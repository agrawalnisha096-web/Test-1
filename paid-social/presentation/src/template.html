Summary

The plan on one page

What we're proposing

  • LinkedIn as the main social channel: finance and operations leaders at businesses with 50–1,000 staff, plus a hand-built list of target companies.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) as a cheaper test: owner-operators with 10–100 staff, local targeting around industrial areas and Brisbane North, and retargeting of everyone who visits the website.
  • Sector-specific ads using our real dashboard screens: hospitality, clinics, logistics, facilities, early learning, mining and civil.
  • Dominic as the face of it: short videos, LinkedIn posts and direct messages sent in his name.

Why it should pay

  • One scoping engagement (A$3,500*) covers about 7 weeks of LinkedIn or 3 months of Meta.
  • One build (A$57k+*) covers about a year of all three channels together.
  • Sales take time, so the 8-week test is judged on cost per qualified lead and cost per call held, not revenue.
  • Clear rules: what doesn't work is stopped at week 4 or 6, and what works gets the budget.

* Prices to be confirmed by Qubik.

Approach

Why social needs a different approach from Google

On Google, people are already searching for "business process automation". On LinkedIn and Instagram, nobody is. A cold "book a call" ad gets very few leads, so we warm people up first and ask for the call once they've shown interest.

Step 1 · Stop the scroll

Name their problem

"Which of your venues made money last fortnight?" The first line names the type of business, so the right people stop and the wrong ones scroll past.

Step 2 · A small first step

Something useful

A playbook, the staff-cost calculator, a sample dashboard for their industry, or a short video from Dominic.

Step 3 · The ask

Free 30-minute call

Shown again to people who engaged: free call → see a working prototype → then decide. Every lead gets a call within an hour.

Review and submit

How the form protects lead quality

  • "Higher intent" form: people review their answers before submitting, which cuts accidental and junk enquiries.
  • Staff question: businesses under 10 staff get a polite thank-you and useful resources instead of a call offer.
  • Typed company name: a simple filter for low-effort submissions.

What happens after someone enquires

  1. The lead reaches Dominic and the CRM within minutes, in the same format as website enquiries.
  2. An SMS goes out straight away: "Thanks, Dominic's team at Qubik will call you today."
  3. A call within one business hour. Social leads cool off fast; speed matters more than anything else in this plan.
  4. Every lead is rated (qualified? call booked? call held?), so we optimise for real conversations, not form fills.

Tracking

Meta and LinkedIn tracking is added to the landing pages, and every ad carries a tag naming the exact ad. Each enquiry email shows which ad and audience it came from. Later, qualified leads are fed back to Meta and LinkedIn so their systems learn what a good lead looks like.

LinkedIn

LinkedIn strategy

LinkedIn reaches finance and operations decision-makers precisely, by job title, company size and industry, and even by named company. It costs more per lead, but more of those leads qualify.

Who we target

Core audience

  • Australia · companies with 11 to 5,000 staff
  • Owners, founders, CEOs/MDs, GMs, CFOs, finance directors and controllers, COOs and heads of operations
  • Industries: logistics and transport, hospitality, health care, facilities services, retail, childcare, mining, civil, aviation
  • Excluded: IT, software, consulting, accounting and recruitment firms

Plus: the "Qubik 500"

A hand-built list of named target companies (see Target list). We upload it to LinkedIn and combine it with senior job titles, so the ads reach decision-makers at exactly the companies we want.

Dominic's personal follow-up

LinkedIn shows which target companies are engaging with the ads. Dominic then sends 10–15 personal connection requests a day to people at those companies, each mentioning something real about their business, with no pitch. The order matters: 2–3 weeks of ads first, then Dominic's note, then a sponsored message only to those who didn't reply.

Beyond standard ads

Creative plays

Reach them at the right moment

  • Hiring admin staff: companies advertising for accounts payable or data entry roles see "Hiring another AP officer? Talk to us first."
  • Opening new sites: "Opening site number 6? Your reporting has to scale with it."
  • New CFO or COO: a note from Dominic within 30–60 days of their move.
  • Using Power BI or spreadsheets: "Built a Power BI dashboard nobody updates?"

Timing

  • Days 1–15 of each month: month-end hooks ("It's the 18th. Do you know last month's profit yet?")
  • Sunday evenings (Meta): "Doing the numbers on a Sunday night again?"
  • Now to mid-December: "Before 2027: get your numbers off spreadsheets."
  • 20 Dec–10 Jan: video views only, to build cheap retargeting audiences for January.
  • April–June: "Don't do another EOFY on spreadsheets."
