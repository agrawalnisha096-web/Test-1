## 1. How the funnel works

One sequence, two channels:
- **Email** carries the story, the value and the offers.
- **SMS** goes only to people who have shown interest, or who are the highest-value segments. It's used for deadlines and reminders.

Every message points to one place: an offer page on qubik.com.au where people claim the offer or book an online review. There are no outbound calls. People come to Qubik when they're ready.

| Stage | What happens | Moves on when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Clean and prepare | Audit, segment, verify emails, set up the sending domain | List is clean and segmented |
| 2. Re-introduce | Dominic's reel and a short "here's what we do now" email; no hard offer | Opens, clicks or replies show who is still interested |
| 3. Give value | Practical, seasonal tax and cash-flow tips | Clicks tag people by interest |
| 4. Offer | Black Friday, then Christmas, then New Year offers, each with a deadline | Claim or booking |
| 5. Convert | Online review held; engagement letter sent | Signed client |
| 6. Nurture or sunset | Monthly email for the engaged; anyone with no opens or clicks after 4 emails is removed | EOFY campaign in April–May |

**Rules the automation follows:**
- **A claim or booking** stops all offer messages to that person.
- **A reply** pauses the sequence, and a person answers within one business day.
- **An unsubscribe or STOP** removes them from every channel immediately.
- **A bounce** removes the address.
- **No opens or clicks after 4 emails:** they're moved to a sunset list, which protects deliverability.

## 2. Segmentation

**Step 1: clean the list.**
- Remove duplicates, current clients, bounced and invalid emails, and role addresses such as info@ or accounts@ where a named contact exists.
- Run the remaining emails through a verification tool.

**Step 2: tag each contact on six fields.** These are all available from the export or simple rules:

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| Relationship | Former client · enquiry that didn't convert · other contact |
| Entity | Business (company, trust, partnership) · sole trader · individual |
| Recency | Last contact within 12 months · 1–3 years · over 3 years |
| Interest (from the original enquiry) | Tax return · BAS and bookkeeping · business advisory · business set-up · other or unknown |
| Size, if known | Revenue or staff from the enquiry or past file |
| Channels and consent | Email and mobile · email only · mobile only; consent recorded or inferred from a past enquiry or client relationship |

**Step 3: assign each contact to one segment.** The shares are estimates to replace after the audit.

| Segment | Who | Est. share | Channels | Lead offer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 Former clients** | Left Qubik in the last 3 years | ~5% | Email + SMS | "Welcome back": first-year discount and a free handover from their current accountant |
| **S2 Recent business enquiries** | Businesses that enquired in the last 12 months | ~10% | Email + SMS | Black Friday package offer |
| **S3 Older business enquiries** | Businesses, 1–3 years | ~20% | Email; SMS once engaged | Black Friday package offer, then the New Year switch offer |
| **S4 Sole traders and individuals** | Tax returns and simple BAS | ~25% | Email; SMS once engaged | Fixed-price tax return and BAS bundle |
| **S5 Dormant** | Any type, over 3 years, still with consent | ~25% | Email only, reduced frequency | Re-introduction only; offers only after they engage |
| **S6 No consent or unreachable** | No consent record, or only a phone number without consent | ~15% | No email or SMS; Meta custom audience only (optional) | Reel and offer shown as ads |

**Step 4: tag by behaviour after each send.** Contacts move between tiers as they act:
- **Hot:** clicked an offer.
- **Warm:** opened or clicked any email.
- **Cold:** no activity.

Only Hot and Warm contacts get SMS (S1 and S2 receive it from the start).

## 5. Thresholds

| Signal (per send) | Keep going | Change something | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Click rate | 2%+ | 1–2%: change the subject line, offer or format | Under 1% across two sends: rethink the offer |
| Bounces | Under 2% | 2–5%: re-verify the segment | Over 5%: pause and clean |
| Spam complaints | Under 0.1% | 0.1–0.3%: slow down and tighten segments | Over 0.3%: stop the segment |
| Unsubscribes | Under 1% | 1–2%: reduce frequency | Over 2%: stop the segment and review the message |
| Offer claims or bookings | 1%+ of contacts reached | 0.5–1%: sharpen the offer | Under 0.5% after Black Friday: replace the offer for Christmas |

Open rates are only shown for reference. Apple Mail inflates them, so clicks, claims and bookings decide.
