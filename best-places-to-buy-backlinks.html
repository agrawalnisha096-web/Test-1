For each provider we then read its own pricing and guarantee pages and recorded the price, model and guarantee with the date checked. We did not buy test links for this guide, and we don't claim to have.

Safety

Is it safe to buy backlinks?

Buying links that pass ranking credit breaks Google's spam policies, so the risk is real, but it is managed by buying editorial placements on real sites rather than links from sites built to sell them. Google classes "buying or selling links for ranking purposes" as link spam and asks for paid links to carry rel="sponsored" (Google spam policies).

Since its December 2022 link spam update, Google uses its SpamBrain system to neutralise unnatural links automatically. In most cases bad links are ignored rather than penalised, which means the money spent on them is simply wasted.

  • Lower risk: articles on real sites with real organic traffic, written for that site's readers, with natural anchor text.
  • Higher risk: sites whose traffic is near zero despite a high DR, "write for us" pages covering every niche, sitewide or footer links, and bulk packages.

Checklist

How to vet any backlink seller

Ask these seven questions before paying anyone, including us.

  1. Can I see the site before I pay? If not, you're buying a tier, not a link.
  2. What's your minimum organic traffic per site? Ask for the number and how it's checked.
  3. Is the link inside an article, and dofollow? Footer, sidebar and sitewide links carry little value.
  4. Who writes the content? It should be written for that site's readers, not stuffed with keywords.
  5. What happens if the link drops? Look for a replacement period of 6 to 12 months.
  6. Do the sites sell links to anyone? "Write for us" pages covering every niche are a warning sign.
  7. Can I see recent live examples? Check their traffic yourself in Ahrefs or Semrush.

Walk away if: DR is high but traffic is near zero, prices are under $50 for "DR 70" sites, or the seller won't name a single site.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to buy backlinks?

It carries risk: Google treats paid links that pass ranking credit as link spam. The risk is lowest with editorial placements on real sites with real traffic and natural anchors, and highest with cheap bulk links and link networks.

How much do backlinks cost in 2026?

Quality guest posts cost about $75 to $400 each at the providers on this list. Niche edits run about $60 to $450. Managed link building plans on this list start between $250 and $1,000 a month.

What is the cheapest safe place to buy backlinks?

PressWhizz has the lowest price on this list, with guest posts from $35, but at that price you vet every site yourself. If you want the vetting done for you, OutreachZ (DA20+) and Rhino Rank (curated links) both start at $60, and Rhino Rank guest posts start at $75 with 12-month replacement cover.

Should I use a marketplace or an agency?

Use a marketplace if you have time to vet sites and want control. Use an agency if you want strategy and accountability and would rather pay more per link than manage outreach.

Can Google detect paid links?

Often, yes. Google's SpamBrain system looks for patterns such as sites that link out to many unrelated businesses, unnatural anchor text and link networks. Detected links are usually ignored, so the money is wasted.

How many backlinks should I buy per month?

There's no safe fixed number. Grow links at a pace that matches your site's size and your competitors' link growth, and prioritise a few relevant, high-traffic placements over many weak ones.
