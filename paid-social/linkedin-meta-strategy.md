# Qubik: LinkedIn and Meta test plan (parallel to Google Ads)

**Written:** 15 Nov 2026. **Status:** proposal, nothing built yet. **Layer 2** (target list, LinkedIn DM ads, Meta location plays, creative testing): `creative-campaign-playbook.md`.
**Offer being sold:** free 30-minute discovery call → fixed-fee scoping and working prototype (A$3,500 founding price, A$7,500 later; *confirm*) → fixed-price build (A$57–90k in the earlier strategy; *confirm*) → optional monthly support.
**Buyer:** owners, CEOs/MDs, CFOs, COOs and GMs of established Australian businesses with 10 to 1,000+ staff, especially multi-site operators (hospitality, healthcare clinics, logistics, facilities services, retail, early learning, mining and civil, aviation services).

All cost figures below are planning estimates built from public 2026 benchmarks (mostly US and agency data; Australian figures are thin). Replace them with our own numbers after four weeks.

---

## 1. The one idea everything else follows from

**Google catches people who are already looking. LinkedIn and Meta interrupt people who are not.**

On Google, "book a free call" works because the searcher already typed "business process automation". A CFO scrolling LinkedIn or a hospitality-group owner on Instagram at 9pm has no such intent. If we show them the same "book a call" ad, we pay for attention and get almost no leads.

So the social channels need:

1. **A smaller first step** that is still worth something on its own (a playbook, a calculator result, a sample dashboard for their industry, a short live demo). It captures people at "this is my problem", before "I'm ready for a call".
2. **Creative that does the targeting.** "Run three or more venues?" in the first line filters the audience better than any targeting setting, especially on Meta.
3. **Retargeting that then asks for the call.** People who opened the playbook, watched half of Dominic's video or used the calculator see the discovery-call offer next.
4. **Fast follow-up.** A lead from a native form forgets about it within hours. Call within an hour during business hours, the same day at the latest.

**Who each channel is for:**

| | LinkedIn | Meta (Facebook + Instagram) |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | 50–1,000 staff; CFO, COO, finance and operations leaders; groups with boards | 10–100 staff; owner-operators who run the business themselves (venues, clinics, childcare centres, trades, transport) |
| Targeting | Exact: job title, seniority, company size, industry, company lists | Loose: detailed targeting is treated as a suggestion; the creative does the work |
| Cost | Expensive: CPM ≈ A$50–90, CPC ≈ A$10–20 | Cheap: CPM ≈ A$12–30, CPC ≈ A$1.50–5 |
| Lead quality | Higher, but CPL ≈ A$150–350 | Mixed; CPL ≈ A$50–150, with many unqualified leads to filter |
| Role in the plan | Main social channel for this offer | Cheaper test for the owner-operator end, plus low-cost retargeting of everyone who visits the site |

**Recommendation:** run both, but treat LinkedIn as the main bet and Meta as a cheaper test with stricter kill rules.

## 2. Budget (recommended test: 8 weeks)

| Channel | Daily | Monthly | 8-week test | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google Ads (running now) | A$45 | ~A$1,370 | ~A$2,500 | No change |
| LinkedIn | A$65 | ~A$2,000 | ~A$4,000 | No more than 3 campaigns live at once at this budget |
| Meta | A$40 | ~A$1,200 | ~A$2,400 | 1 cold campaign + 1 retargeting campaign |
| **Total** | **A$150** | **~A$4,570** | **~A$8,900** | |

**Lean option** if that is too much: LinkedIn A$40/day + Meta A$25/day (≈ A$2,000/month extra). This works, but each test takes about twice as long to give a clear answer.

**Why not less:** LinkedIn's cost per thousand impressions in Australia is about A$50–90. At A$65/day that buys roughly 750–1,300 impressions a day. Much below about A$40/day, there aren't enough leads in a month to tell a good angle from a bad one.

**Also budget for creative:** Dominic's time for about 2 hours of phone-shot video and 4–6 LinkedIn posts. Optionally a freelance editor (≈ A$300–600 for a batch of short edits).

## 3. Expected results and ROI (estimates)

### Funnel assumptions
| Step | Native form (LinkedIn/Meta) | Landing page form |
|---|---|---|
| Lead → qualified (right size, real problem) | LinkedIn 55–70% · Meta 25–40% | 60–75% |
| Qualified lead → call held | 50–65% | 65–80% |
| Call held → scoping sold | 15–25% | 15–25% |
| Scoping → build | 40–60% | 40–60% |

### 8-week test
| | LinkedIn (A$4,000) | Meta (A$2,400) |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per lead | A$150–350 | A$50–150 |
| Leads | 11–27 | 16–48 |
| Qualified leads | 7–17 | 5–17 |
| Calls held | 4–10 | 3–9 |
| Scoping sold | 1–2 | 0–2 |
| Builds (usually month 3–6, not inside the test) | 0–1 | 0–1 |

### What makes it pay
- **One scoping sale (A$3,500) pays for about seven weeks of LinkedIn, or about three months of Meta.**
- **One build (A$57k+) pays for about a year of all three channels together.** The real return comes from builds, so the business case is: across all channels, can we sell **at least one build every 6 months**? Combined spend over six months is about A$27k.
- Sales cycles for a A$57–90k project are long: 1–3 months from first call to signed scoping, and more to the build. **Judge the 8-week test on leading indicators, not revenue:**

| Metric (targets) | LinkedIn | Meta |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per qualified lead | ≤ A$450 | ≤ A$300 |
| Cost per call held | ≤ A$800 | ≤ A$600 |
| CTR (cold) | ≥ 0.5% (single image), ≥ 1% (Thought Leader Ads) | ≥ 1% (link CTR) |
| Lead form completion rate | ≥ 10% of opens | ≥ 8% of clicks |

**How confident:** low on exact numbers (the Australian benchmarks are thin and agencies disagree by 2–3×), medium on the shape (LinkedIn costs more per lead but more of them qualify; Meta is cheaper but needs filtering).

## 5. LinkedIn plan

### Account setup
- Campaign Manager ad account in **AUD**, linked to the Qubik Company Page. Vinay needs **Super Admin** (or Content Admin) on the Page to sponsor posts; Dominic must approve Thought Leader Ads of his posts.
- **LinkedIn Insight Tag** on all landing pages and thank-you pages (see section 8).
- Conversions: "Lead – landing page" (thank-you page visit), "Lead – LinkedIn form" (automatic).
- **Turn off** audience expansion and the LinkedIn Audience Network on every campaign.

### Core audience ("ICP – Australia")
- **Location:** Australia (permanent location).
- **Company size:** 11–50, 51–200, 201–500, 501–1,000, 1,001–5,000.
- **Two ways in, tested as two audiences:**
  - **Seniority + function:** seniority Owner, Partner, CXO, VP, Director; job functions Finance, Operations, General Business (shown as "Business Development" in some lists; check what Campaign Manager offers).
  - **Job titles:** Owner, Founder, Managing Director, CEO, General Manager, CFO, Finance Director, Financial Controller, Head of Finance, COO, Operations Director, Head of Operations.
- **Industries:** Transportation/Logistics, Truck Transportation, Warehousing; Hospitality, Food & Beverage Services, Restaurants; Hospitals & Health Care, Medical Practices; Facilities Services; Retail; Child Day Care Services / Education (centres); Mining; Civil Engineering / Construction; Airlines & Aviation.
- **Exclusions:** IT services and consulting, software development, management consulting, accounting (competitors), staffing and recruiting, government administration; Qubik employees; existing Qubik leads (uploaded list).
- **Likely size:** about 40,000–150,000 members. Fine for an 8-week test. If it's under 30,000, add more industries before narrowing anything else.

### Campaigns (weeks 1–8)

| # | Campaign | Objective / format | Daily | Audience | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LI-1 | **Cold – Playbook (Document Ad)** | Lead generation · Document Ad with a gated LinkedIn form after page 3 | A$30 | Core ICP | Main lead source. People read the first 3 pages free, then fill in a form pre-filled with their profile to get the rest. No website needed. |
| LI-2 | **Cold – Dominic (Thought Leader Ads)** | Engagement or website visits · Dominic's own posts, sponsored | A$15 | Core ICP | Cheapest reach, and it builds the retargeting pool. Founder posts usually get 2–3× the CTR of company ads. |
| LI-3 | **Warm – Free call (from week 3)** | Lead generation · single image + LinkedIn form, or website conversions | A$20 | Retargeting: document openers, people who watched 50%+ of a video, people who engaged with Dominic's posts, website visitors (90 days), Company Page followers | Asks for the discovery call. Uses A7, A8 and the testimonial. |

Until the retargeting pool reaches LinkedIn's 300-member minimum (likely 2–3 weeks), LI-3's A$20/day goes to LI-1.

**LI-1 Document Ad: the playbook.** Working title: *"The Weekly Numbers Playbook: the 6 numbers every multi-site business should see every Monday, and how to stop building them by hand."* 8–10 pages:
1. The cost of manual reporting (calculator maths).
2. The 6 numbers (profit by site, labour %, margin trend, cash, forward view, exceptions), using real dashboard screens with sample data.
3. Sector pages: what those numbers look like in hospitality, clinics, logistics and facilities.
4. "What to automate first": supplier bills, orders, the reporting pack.
5. A self-check: 10 yes/no questions on how ready you are.
6. How Qubik works, the prototype-before-build promise, and an invitation to the free call.

**Gated form fields:** name, email, company, job title (pre-filled), plus 2 custom questions: **staff numbers** and **"When would you want this sorted?"** (Now / This quarter / Within 6 months / Just exploring). That's enough to sort leads without hurting the completion rate.

**Phase 2 for LinkedIn (weeks 9+, if phase 1 works):**
- **Sector Document Ads:** one playbook per winning sector.
- **Company list targeting (ABM):** a list of 300–1,000 named Australian companies that fit (multi-site groups, 50–500 staff), built from public lists and LinkedIn Sales Navigator, and targeted with all three campaigns.
- **LinkedIn Live event / webinar:** "Inside a live profit dashboard: a 30-minute walk-through with Dominic", promoted with Event Ads. Registrations make good warm leads.
- **Conversation Ads from Dominic,** only to the company list. They're expensive, so only for a list of known good-fit companies.

## 6. Meta plan

### Account setup
- Meta Business Portfolio with the Qubik Facebook Page and Instagram account, an ad account in AUD and the **Meta Pixel** (dataset) on all landing pages (section 8).
- **Domain verification** for qubik.com.au: easiest with a DNS TXT record (the developer already handling DNS for `send.qubik.com.au` can add it), because a WordPress Contributor can't edit the site's `<head>`.
- Placements: Facebook and Instagram feeds, Stories and Reels. **Exclude Audience Network** (junk placements for B2B).
- **Special ad category risk:** our ads are about automation and reporting, not financial products, so they shouldn't need the "Financial products and services" category. If Meta forces it, targeting is limited to location only. That's survivable because the creative does the targeting anyway.

### Audiences
- **Cold:** Australia, age 30–65, Advantage+ audience. Suggestions (not hard limits): small-business owners, business page admins, Xero/MYOB/accounting software interests, and industry interests (hospitality industry, restaurant management, logistics, childcare, allied health). Expect Meta to go wider than this; the hook and the qualifying questions keep quality up.
- **Warm:** all qubik.com.au visitors in the last 180 days (including Google Ads landing-page visitors), people who watched 50%+ of our videos, people who engaged with the Page or Instagram, and people who opened the form without submitting.
- **Existing Qubik accounting clients (customer list):** see section 7. This is likely the best-converting audience on any channel.

### Campaigns

| # | Campaign | Objective / format | Daily | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M-1 | **Cold – Instant form (higher intent)** | Leads · Instant Form, "Higher intent" type (adds a review step) | A$28 | 4–6 ads: A1, A4 (hospitality), A4 (clinics or logistics), A5 and A9. Mostly Dominic videos (30–45s, shot on a phone) plus 2 statics using dashboard screens. |
| M-2 | **Warm – Retargeting** | Leads (landing page) or Instant Form | A$12 | A7, A8, the testimonial, the calculator and the free call. Cheap reach to everyone who has visited from Google, LinkedIn or Meta. |

**Week 4 test:** Instant Form vs **calculator landing page** (section 7, page P2) in M-1, split 50/50. Instant forms nearly always give a lower cost per lead; the landing page often gives a better cost per *qualified* lead. That's the number that decides the winner.

**Instant Form questions** (higher intent):
1. "How many staff do you have?" (Under 10 / 10–50 / 51–200 / 200+). **"Under 10" goes to a polite end screen without a call offer** (still a lead, but low priority).
2. "What's costing you most right now?" (Manual admin and data entry / Slow month-end reporting / No clear view of profit by site / Systems that don't talk / Something else)
3. "When would you want this sorted?" (Now / This quarter / Within 6 months / Just exploring)
4. Name, email, mobile (pre-filled), company name (typed: an extra filter for junk).
- **Intro screen:** "A free 30-minute call with business experts who understand both the numbers and the technology. No obligation."
- **End screen:** "We'll call you within one business day" + a button to the landing page.

## 8. Tracking and lead handling (must be ready before spending)

1. **LinkedIn Insight Tag and Meta Pixel** added to the existing "Tracking code" blocks on pages 588 and 589 (and on any new pages). Every `<script>` gets `data-no-optimize="1"`; no `&&` or `<` in inline JS, as with the Google tag.
2. **Lead event on the thank-you page:** Meta `Lead` (with advanced matching from the same `qlp_lead` sessionStorage values the Google enhanced conversion uses, sending the same event ID) and a LinkedIn conversion.
3. **Click IDs:** extend the page script to also capture `fbclid` and `li_fat_id` into the hidden form (next to gclid). This lets us send qualified leads and sales back to Meta and LinkedIn later (offline conversions / Conversions API, through Make).
4. **UTMs on every ad:** `utm_source=linkedin|meta`, `utm_medium=paid-social`, `utm_campaign=<campaign id>`, `utm_content=<angle>-<format>` (for example `a4-hosp-video`). The form already records them, so every lead email shows which angle produced it.
5. **Native form leads (LinkedIn forms, Meta Instant Forms)** never touch the website, so the Divi email doesn't fire. Route them through **Make.com** (already planned for the CRM): LinkedIn Lead Gen and Facebook Lead Ads modules → email to Dominic and Vinay plus the CRM address, in the same layout as the website leads. Until that's live, download leads daily; no lead should sit for more than a few hours.
6. **Speed to lead:** call within 1 business hour. Send an SMS/email straight away: "Thanks [name], Dominic's team will call you today from (07) 3205 8938."
7. **Self-reported source on the call:** "How did you hear about us?" Social ads also drive Google brand searches and direct visits that the platforms won't get credit for.
8. **Privacy policy:** add LinkedIn and Meta tracking and lead forms (someone with Editor access needs to do this).

### Kill / keep rules
- **Week 4, per channel:** cost per qualified lead more than 2× target, with no call held → change the offer (not just the creative). If still bad at week 6 → pause the channel.
- **Ad level:** after spend of about 2× target CPL with no lead → turn it off. CTR below half the target after 3,000 impressions → change the hook.
- **Meta lead quality:** if more than 60% of leads are unqualified → switch to the landing page (P2) or add a harder qualifying question (revenue).
- **Scale:** the winning channel gets the other channel's budget before any new money is added.
