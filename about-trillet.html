The problem

The calls that matter most are the ones automation keeps dropping.

Every organisation runs two kinds of conversations. The simple ones — a hold time, an FAQ, a status check. And the high-stakes ones: verifying who someone is, taking a payment, booking a critical appointment, handling a matter that's sensitive, regulated, or worth a great deal.

The first kind, most tools can handle. The second kind is where they fall apart. Generic voice bots can talk, but they can't verify, can't act inside your systems, and can't be trusted when compliance is on the line. So the hardest, most valuable calls still land on stretched teams — or go unanswered.

Our insight

A voice agent is only useful if it can do the same things your best staff member can: prove who it's talking to, take real action in real systems, and leave a record. Everything else is just a nicer hold message.

Our story

Built by operators, engineers, and people who've run the phones.

Trillet didn't start in a lab. It started with founders who'd each spent years inside high-stakes conversations — from three different angles — and kept reaching the same conclusion: the technology wasn't good enough for the calls that mattered most.

Put those three perspectives in a room and the product designs itself. Trillet is what you get when the person who's run the contact centre, the person who's built the critical systems, and the person who's scaled the voice AI decide to build the thing none of them could buy.

Common questions

What buyers ask us first.

What does Trillet actually do?+

Trillet is a voice-AI platform that resolves high-stakes conversations end-to-end. Its agents verify a caller's identity, connect to your live business systems, take real actions — bookings, payments, case updates — and follow through across voice, SMS, chat, and email, leaving a full audit trail.

How is Trillet different from other voice AI platforms?+

Most voice AI can hold a conversation. Trillet is built to complete one — to verify, act inside real systems, and close the loop to compliance standards. It's designed for regulated, high-stakes use from the ground up, rather than adding compliance as an enterprise add-on.

Which industries do you serve?+

Healthcare, legal, financial services and collections, government, utilities, and automotive & retail — anywhere a dropped call or an unverified caller carries real cost.

Is Trillet secure and compliant?+

Trillet supports HIPAA, GDPR, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, TCPA, and ACMA requirements, with full audit trails on every interaction. It can be deployed on-premise, in a private cloud, or fully managed, with Australian data residency available.

Can Trillet integrate with our existing systems?+

Yes. Trillet connects to the CRM, practice-management, payment, and calendar systems your teams already use, orchestrating voice, SMS, chat, and email from a single trigger.

Who is behind Trillet, and where are you based?+

Trillet was founded in Melbourne, Australia in 2024 by Laurence Latin (CTO) and Ming Xu (COO), and is led by CEO Rob Petreski. We're proudly Australian and globally operated.
