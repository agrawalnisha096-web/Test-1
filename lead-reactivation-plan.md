## 1. Next steps

1. **Export and audit (days 1–3).**
   - Pull the full list with its source, date captured, last contact and consent fields.
   - Remove duplicates, bounced and invalid emails, and existing clients.
   - Check mobile numbers against the Do Not Call Register before any calls.
2. **Segment (days 3–5).**
   - **A: recent and good fit** (contacted in the last 2 years, matches the ideal customer). A person calls them.
   - **B: older, but still a fit.** Email and SMS, plus Meta retargeting.
   - **C: very old, poor fit, or no record of consent.** Meta custom audience only, or excluded.
3. **Consent check.**
   - Email and SMS go only to people who gave express consent, or where consent can reasonably be inferred from a past enquiry or business relationship.
   - Every message identifies the business and carries a working unsubscribe, actioned within 5 working days (ACMA).
   - Anyone uncertain goes into the Meta custom audience rather than the inbox, provided the privacy policy covers it.
   - *Confirm this with the business's privacy adviser.*
4. **Message and offer.**
   - A short, personal note from ⟦founder/name⟧ carrying the reel: "It's been a while. Here's what we're doing now [60-second reel]. Want ⟦offer, e.g. a free 30-minute review⟧?"
   - Three ways to respond: reply, use the booking link, or wait for a call from the team.
5. **Send in waves.**
   - **Wave 1** (days 8–14): about 600 contacts, segment A and the best of B. This tests the message on a part of the list before using all of it.
   - **Waves 2–3** (days 15–30): the rest of the contactable list, using whatever worked in wave 1.
6. **Retargeting.** Upload all contactable leads to Meta as a custom audience (hashed). Show them the reel for 30 days, then a second, offer-led ad to anyone who watched at least half of it.
7. **Follow-up.** Two follow-ups over 10 days. People who still don't respond get a useful monthly email (a tip, a short case, a new offer), never the same pitch again.

## 2. Thresholds per wave

| Signal | Keep going | Change something | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reply or booking rate | 2%+ | 1–2%: rewrite the message or offer | Under 1% |
| Unsubscribes and complaints | Under 1% | — | Over 1%: stop and review the list and message |
| Email bounces | Under 3% | 3–5%: clean the list | Over 5%, or any spam block: stop email and fix the sending domain |

Send from a **separate sending domain**, so the main domain's email reputation is protected while old addresses are tested.
