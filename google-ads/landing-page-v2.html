In plain English

What is business process automation?

Most businesses have jobs that follow the same steps every time: open the email, type the invoice into Xero, check it against the order, chase someone if it doesn't match.

Automation is software that does those steps for you, inside the systems you already use. It works around the clock, doesn't get bored, and doesn't make typing mistakes. Your team stays in charge.

It reads

Emails, PDFs, forms and spreadsheets, the way a person would.

It checks

Against your orders, price lists and rules.

It acts

Posts to Xero, updates your systems, sends the email or the report.

And asks a person when it isn't sure

Anything unusual goes to your team to decide, with the details attached.

Questions

Questions we get asked

We're not a tech business. Is this for us?

Yes. Automation is built for businesses whose team spends time on repetitive admin, not for tech companies. You don't need technical staff: we handle the build and explain everything in plain English.

How do we know what's worth automating?

That's what the free call and the assessment are for. We look at where your team's time goes and what each process costs, then recommend the ones that pay back fastest.

Will it work with our software?

Usually. We work with Xero, MYOB and most tools that have email, an online login or an export. We'll check your setup on the call.

Do we need IT staff to run it?

No. Your team sees the results in the systems they already use, and approves anything the automation isn't sure about.

What happens if something goes wrong?

Anything uncertain goes to a person rather than being guessed. With ongoing support, we monitor the automations and fix issues as your systems change.

Is our data safe? Is AI involved?

Your data stays in your own systems; we only access what the automation needs. Where AI is used, it's for reading documents and drafting replies, with a person in control.

What does it cost?

It depends on the processes involved, so we don't publish packages. See how pricing works above; you'll always know the price before any work starts.

How long does it take?

It depends on the process and your systems. The assessment gives you a timeline before you commit to anything.

What happens on the call?

A relaxed 30-minute conversation by phone or video. We ask how your business runs, you ask us anything, and we tell you honestly whether automation is worth it for you.
