# AGENTS.md — Handoff for the next AI agent (Trillet marketing assets)

This branch (`claude/trillet-about-page-research-yd7jab`) contains marketing and SEO
deliverables for **Trillet** (trillet.ai). It is a content/deliverables branch, not an
application codebase. There is no build system, no tests, and no framework — just
standalone HTML, Markdown, generated `.docx`/`.pdf` files, and CSVs. Read this whole
file before editing anything.

---

## 1. Project summary

Trillet (also styled "Trillet AI") is a **Melbourne-based enterprise voice AI company**.
Its platform resolves high-stakes, regulated phone/messaging conversations end to end:
its **voice AI agents** verify a caller's identity, act inside the customer's systems of
record (CRM, payments, scheduling), and follow through across voice, SMS, chat and email,
leaving a full audit trail. The work on this branch is for **Trillet's marketing/growth
function** (the human owner is the requester; their contact email is recorded only inside
`trillet-offpage-submission-kit.docx`). The branch began as an SEO audit (the `seo-audit/`
folder, done earlier) and grew to include a full **About page** (design + copy) and an
**off-page directory/marketplace submission kit**. Goal: give Trillet publish-ready,
accurate, non-AI-sounding marketing assets grounded only in verifiable facts.

**Decisions and WHY (including things discussed but not in any file):**
- **Do NOT publish funding/valuation figures.** Aggregators (e.g. startuphub.ai) list
  "$50M raised / $130M valuation / $388M" and even "Tel Aviv" and a "Laurence Latin
  founder-only" framing — these are a **name collision** with unrelated companies **"Rillet"**
  (AI ERP) and **"Triller"** (social app). All rejected as unreliable. Do not reintroduce.
- **Do NOT name "Bland" / Bland.ai.** Ming's bio originally referenced being "Bland.ai's
  first partner"; the client asked to remove the name, so it now reads "one of the earliest
  partners of a leading voice platform." Keep it anonymised.
- **Positioning = "resolves high-stakes, regulated conversations end to end,"** not the older
  "outbound sales follow-ups / your leads ignore you" framing that still floats around in
  older marketing. Use the current, enterprise framing.
- **About page flow was reordered for enterprise buyers:** problem → how it works →
  measurable outcomes → security/governance → technology → comparison → industries →
  story/leadership → mission → values → milestones → press → FAQ → contact → CTA. Rationale:
  a CIO/CISO/ops buyer scans for "what it does, outcomes, is it safe/governed" before the
  founder story. (Earlier versions led with the founder story; rejected as too soft for
  enterprise.)
- **"Why we exist" and "Leadership" were merged** into one section (each founder shown once
  with a vantage label + perspective + full bio) to remove duplication.
- **Language was deliberately de-"AI-sounding":** the client explicitly asked to remove
  **em dashes**, **"not X, but Y" negations**, and **forceful/hype phrasing**. This was
  applied across the HTML, both handoff files, and the submission kit. KEEP IT THIS WAY.
  Examples of the required style: "Resolution over conversation" (not "Resolution, not
  conversation"); "Built to pass your security review." (not "…not just your pilot");
  "Let's talk about your hardest conversations." (not "Bring us your hardest conversations").
- **Spelling: "voice AI" (two words, no hyphen), and "voice AI agents"** is the preferred
  phrase — the client corrected "voice-AI" → "voice AI" and asked for "voice AI agents" to
  appear in the tagline, descriptions and keywords. Primary tagline is now **"Enterprise
  voice AI agents for high-stakes conversations."**
- **Submission kit: target-list and tracker removed** at client request (already owned).
  Review-generation section also removed at client request.
- **JSON-LD: no self-referential `aggregateRating`** on the Organization schema — Google
  disallows self-serving review markup and it risks a manual action. The 4.6 rating stays
  on-page attributed to Trustpilot only. This is noted in the handoff and should not change.

## 4. Sources and methods

- **Research:** `WebSearch` + `WebFetch` against trillet.ai (home, `/enterprise`, `/ai-info`),
  the Sept 2025 press release, Trustpilot, and Google results for social profiles. The
  founder bios came from **three LinkedIn profile PDFs the client uploaded** (Rob, Laurence,
  Ming) — those PDFs are NOT in the repo; the facts distilled from them live in
  `about-trillet.html` and `about-trillet-handoff.md`.
- **What did NOT work:** LinkedIn blocks automated fetching (HTTP 999) — you cannot scrape
  profiles; rely on the bios already written or ask the client. Plain web search on the
  founders' names collides badly with other people; treat unverified search hits with
  suspicion.
- **`.docx` generation:** both Word files were produced with **docx-js (npm `docx`)** via
  Node build scripts kept in the session scratchpad (`build-docx.js` for the About handoff,
  `build-submission.js` for the submission kit) — **these scripts are NOT committed**, so to
  regenerate the `.docx` you will need to recreate them or edit the `.docx` XML directly
  (unzip → edit `word/document.xml` → zip). See the `docx` skill for the mechanics.
- **Rendering limitation:** this environment had **no working LibreOffice/PDF renderer or
  pdftoppm/pandoc**, so the `.docx` files were validated structurally (valid OOXML, content
  greps) but never visually screenshotted. If you can render them, do a visual pass.
- **Artifact publishing** (the live About-page preview URL) was a Claude-only capability;
  Codex should treat `about-trillet.html` as canonical and ignore the artifact URL.

## 5. Preferences and conventions

- **Tone:** enterprise, calm, precise, outcome/governance-oriented. No hype, no clever
  slogans, no "we're on a mission to revolutionise" language.
- **Hard style rules (the client corrected these — follow exactly):**
  - **No em dashes (—) anywhere.** Use commas, colons, or full stops. (The comparison table
    in the HTML uses "✓"/"✕", not dashes, for the same reason.)
  - **No "not X, but Y" / unnecessary-negation constructions.** State things positively.
  - **No forceful/hype phrasing.**
  - **"voice AI"** (never "voice-AI"); use **"voice AI agents"** in prominent copy.
- **Accuracy over completeness:** never invent figures, certifications, customers, or bios.
  Anything unverified is marked **CONFIRM** in the docs. Keep that discipline.
- **File formats:** the client likes **Word `.docx`** for handoff/reference deliverables and
  a **single self-contained `.html`** for the page. Markdown is used as the editable source
  alongside the docx.
- **Delivery:** work is committed and pushed to this branch; deliverable files are also
  surfaced to the client directly. Commit messages are descriptive and end with the
  Co-Authored-By / Claude-Session trailer lines used on existing commits (match the repo's
  existing style if you add commits).
- **British/Australian spelling** is used in the copy (e.g. "personalisation", "colour",
  "organisations") since Trillet is Australian. Keep it consistent.

## 7. Gotchas

- **The `.docx` files have no committed source.** `build-docx.js` and `build-submission.js`
  lived in the session scratchpad and were not committed. To change a `.docx` you must
  either edit `word/document.xml` inside the zip directly, or rewrite a build script.
  Do NOT assume a committed generator exists.
- **Keep the em-dash / no-negation / no-hype rules on every edit.** It is easy to
  reintroduce an em dash or a "not just… but…" phrase. Re-grep for `—` after any copy edit.
  ("voice-AI" should also always be zero.)
- **Never reintroduce the `$50M/$130M/$388M` funding numbers, "Tel Aviv", or the Bland/
  Bland.ai name.** These were explicitly rejected (name collision / client request).
- **Do not trust LinkedIn scraping or bare name searches for the founders** — LinkedIn
  returns HTTP 999 and names collide with other people.
- **The live artifact URL is not editable by a non-Claude agent** — treat `about-trillet.html`
  as the source of truth; don't try to "update the artifact."
- **This is not an app** — there is nothing to build, run, or test. Don't add a package.json,
  framework, or CI expecting a codebase.
- **`seo-audit/` PDFs v1/v2 are superseded by v3.** Don't cite v1/v2 numbers as current.
- **British/Australian spelling** is intentional; don't "correct" it to US spelling.
