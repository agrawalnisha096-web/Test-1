03

Gaps

Two kinds: structural (hubs that would consolidate existing pages) and content (subjects with little or no coverage).

Structural

  • /security hub — absent (404). Compliance is the core differentiator and has no page that owns it.
  • /compare hub — absent (404). ~70 comparison pages are unindexed by a hub.
  • /enterprise hub — redirects; solution pages are not consolidated.
  • Per-pillar hubs — agency, industry, integrations and resources lack hubs.
  • Vertical-page / blog overlap — /receptionist/industries pages and vertical blog posts target the same terms.
  • Cross-cluster links — the three product lines link to one another weakly.

Content

  • Missing verticals — banking, salon & beauty, e-commerce, education.
  • Integrations breadth — beyond GoHighLevel: CRM, calendar, automation, telephony.
  • Deployment cluster — on-premise, hybrid and data residency are uncollected.
  • Educational concepts — category education (customer-service automation, AI phone systems).
  • Case studies — none present on the site.
  • Partnership education — thin content above the partner-program pages.
04

Prioritisation

Sequenced by structural leverage and by alignment with existing demand.

  1. Build the compliance and comparison hubs.

    Both subjects are unhoused; the compliance hub owns a core differentiator, the comparison hub consolidates the largest existing cluster.

  2. Consolidate the SMB receptionist cluster.

    Largest demand and the clearest striking-distance opportunity; resolve the vertical-page and blog overlap.

  3. Establish an enterprise hub.

    Consolidate the solution pages and add the deployment cluster.

  4. Add hubs and consolidate white-label.

    Agency, industry, integrations and partnership hubs; reduce comparison duplication.

  5. Fill content gaps and wire cross-cluster links.

    Missing verticals, integrations, educational content and case studies; connect the three product lines.
